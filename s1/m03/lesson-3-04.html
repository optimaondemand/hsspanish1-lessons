★ Aplicación — ¿Ser o Estar?
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the rule. Today you use it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the ser vs. estar contrast, and it lands a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how the choice becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. Instead you'll make the choice yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation. It's all ungraded practice: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose ser or estar to complete a sentence and explain why.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken sentence with estar and write down what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The rule, in one breath

Before you write anything, say the shortcut out loud: ser = who or what something is (identity, origin). estar = where it is or how it feels right now (location, condition). Hold that in your head — you're about to make the call yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Yesterday's contrast — how comfortable do you feel with it right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You make the choice

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank with the correct form of ser (soy, eres, es, somos, son) or estar (estoy, estás, está, estamos, están). Say why to yourself as you write. Then commit to all six before you reveal the key — no peeking early; the thinking is the practice.

SER — who / what

Identity, origin, lasting traits.
soy · eres · es · somos · son

ESTAR — where / how now

Location and right-now feelings.
estoy · estás · está · estamos · están
  1. Yo ______ de Puerto Rico.
  2. Nosotros ______ en la escuela ahora.
  3. Mi madre ______ ocupada hoy.
  4. Tú ______ estudiante.
  5. Ellos ______ contentos.
  6. La casa ______ cerca de la ciudad.
1. soy — origin → ser. (Yo soy de Puerto Rico.)
2. estamos — location → estar. (Nosotros estamos en la escuela.)
3. está — a right-now condition → estar. (Mi madre está ocupada.)
4. eres — identity → ser. (Tú eres estudiante.)
5. están — a feeling → estar. (Ellos están contentos.)
6. está — location → estar. (La casa está cerca de la ciudad.)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two more sentences of your own about your real life today — one that must use ser (who you are / where you're from) and one that must use estar (where you are / how you feel right now). Read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same estar sentences you met yesterday — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your accent marks, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Yo estoy en casa. (I am at home.) — estar for location.
2
Write what you hear…
¿Cómo estás? (How are you?) — note the opening ¿ and the accent on estás.
3
Write what you hear…
Ella está cansada. (She is tired.) — a feeling → estar; cansada agrees with ella.
4
Write what you hear…
Nosotros estamos en la escuela. (We are at school.) — estar for location.
5
Write what you hear…
Ellos están ocupados. (They are busy.) — a right-now condition → estar; accent on están.

Notice every sentence used estar — because each one is about location or a right-now feeling. That's the pattern your ear is learning to expect.

🧠 PART 4 · SER O ESTAR? 8 min

Two to finish on

Same question every time: is this who/what they are (ser), or where/how they are (estar)?

✅ TRY IT · choose the right verb
"Yo ______ preocupado hoy." (I ______ worried today.)
✅ TRY IT · choose the right verb
"Mi profesora ______ de Colombia." (My teacher ______ from Colombia.)

One to build yourself: how would you say "They are at work"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Ellos están en el trabajo. Location → estar; "they" → están.

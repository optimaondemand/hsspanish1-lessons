★ Repaso y espiral — Retrieval Before the Quiz
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
No new grammar today — just pulling it back out of your head, which is where real learning happens.
This is your fourth meeting with ser vs. estar, and the first day of Week 2. Instead of new material, today you retrieve: an ear-quiz on the feelings and places, a short production prompt, and a spiral that puts three verbs side by side — ser (from Módulo 1), regular -ar verbs like estudiar and trabajar (from Módulo 2), and estar (this module). Bringing all three together is exactly what the Day-10 quiz will ask of you.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize spoken feeling and place words and match them to meaning.
2
I will be able to choose among ser, an -ar verb, and estar depending on the job the sentence is doing.

🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · TRES VERBOS 10 min

Three verbs, three jobs

You now know three ways to talk about people. They are not interchangeable — each one does a different job. Listen and read across the row:

Verb (from)Its jobExample · listen
ser (Módulo 1) who / what someone is · origin Yo soy de Cuba.
-ar verb (Módulo 2) an action someone does Yo estudio y trabajo. (estudiar, trabajar)
estar (Módulo 3) where someone is · how they feel now Yo estoy en casa.

Hear all three collide in one sentence: Mi hermano está en el trabajo, pero es profesor. — está (where he is now) and es (who he is) in the same breath.

✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser, -ar, or estar?
"Ana ___ español todos los días." (Ana ___ Spanish every day.) — it's an action.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser, -ar, or estar?
"Nosotros ___ de Puerto Rico." (We ___ from Puerto Rico.) — it's origin.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser, -ar, or estar?
"Yo ___ muy contento hoy." (I ___ very happy today.) — it's a right-now feeling.
✍️ PART 4 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit to an answer before you peek. Then reveal to check your verb choice and spelling. This is a self-test, so being honest with yourself is where the value is.

1. Say: “I am from the United States, but right now I am at school.”

Soy de Estados Unidos, pero ahora estoy en la escuela. Origin → soy (ser); location right now → estoy (estar).

2. Say: “My mom works and she is tired.”

Mi mamá trabaja y está cansada. An action → trabaja (-ar verb); a right-now feeling → está (estar). Note cansada agrees with mamá.

3. Say: “We are students and we are happy.”

Somos estudiantes y estamos contentos/as. Identity → somos (ser); a feeling → estamos (estar). The adjective agrees with the whole group.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER OTRA VEZ 5 min

Sofía returns — listen and read

You met this passage on Day 5. Press play, follow along, and this time notice how ser and estar take turns doing their jobs.

Play the reading

«Hola, me llamo Sofía. Soy de La Habana, Cuba. Ahora estoy en Miami con mi familia. Hoy estoy muy contenta porque no hay clases. Mi hermano está cansado, pero mi madre está ocupada en casa. Nosotros somos cubanos, y siempre estamos juntos.»

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Point to each verb and say its job out loud: soy (who she is), estoy contenta (feeling now), somos cubanos (identity), estamos juntos (a right-now situation). Could you add one sentence about Sofía using an -ar verb? (Hint: Sofía estudia en Miami.)
🌎 CULTURA · EL SPANGLISH DE MIAMI
Una ciudad bilingüe

Miami es una ciudad donde el español y el inglés conviven todos los días. Families like Sofía's often move fluidly between the two languages — a real-world reminder that ser (who a city is) and estar (where it is, on the Florida coast) both describe the same place from different angles.

★ LA VIRTUD · PERSEVERANCE

Retrieval practice feels harder than re-reading — and that difficulty is the point. Every time you pull ser, estar, or an -ar verb out of your memory instead of copying it, you make the pathway stronger. Tú eres capaz, y hoy estás un paso más cerca del examen. Perseverance is choosing the harder, more useful practice.

📖 TODAY IS DIFFERENT
You've done the learning. Today you show it — the first graded work of Módulo 3 lives here.
This is a launch page, not a lesson — there's very little new to read. Week 1 (Lessons 3.01–3.06) built the habit: places, feelings, the verb estar, and the ser vs. estar contrast, all spaced out and recycled. Now two graded activities let you demonstrate it. Read the briefing below, do the quick readiness check, then open each activity in Canvas when you're ready.

📝 PART 2 · EVALUACIÓN 1: VOCABULARY CHECK 13 min

Vocabulary Check · 25 points · 1 attempt

This one is mostly by ear. You'll hear a Spanish word — a place or a feeling from this module — and match it to its meaning or spelling. A few items ask you to recognize the written word instead. Everything comes from Lessons 3.01–3.05.

DetailWhat to know
Points25
Attempts1 — this is a one-shot check, so prepare before you open it.
FormatListening & matching (turn your sound on); a few reading items.
CoversPlaces (la casa, la escuela, el trabajo, la ciudad), position words (aquí, allí, cerca de, lejos de), and feelings (contento, triste, cansado, nervioso, ocupado, enfermo, enojado, aburrido, preocupado).

Tip: use earbuds or headphones if you can, and find a quiet minute — you get one attempt.

▶ To take the Vocabulary Check: return to Module 3 in Canvas and open the Vocabulary Check — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten lessons.
✍️ PART 3 · EVALUACIÓN 2: LENGUA EN ACCIÓN 17 min

Lengua en acción · 20 points · structured writing

Here you produce the language. You'll write a few short, guided sentences about yourself and one other person — where you are, how you feel, who you are — deliberately using both verbs. The prompts tell you exactly what each sentence should do; you supply the Spanish.

The single idea being graded is the choice you've practiced all week:

SER — who / what someone is

Identity & origin.

Yo soy de… (I'm from…)
Mi madre es profesora.

ESTAR — where / how right now

Location & feelings.

Yo estoy en casa.
Hoy estoy contento/a.

Before you open it, hear the target one more time. This is the model sentence your writing should echo — one ser idea, one estar idea, in a single breath:

  • Mi hermano…«Mi hermano está en el trabajo, pero es profesor.»
▶ To complete Lengua en acción: return to Module 3 in Canvas and open the Lengua en acción — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten lessons.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Warm up your ear before you write: replay Sofía's short reading from Lesson 3.05 and count how many times she uses ser versus estar. Then borrow her rhythm in your own sentences.
🧩 PART 4 · CÓMO PREPARARTE 5 min

Not sure yet? Prepare in five minutes

If your readiness check said “almost” or “not yet,” do these before you open either activity — in this order:

1
Run the flip-cards. Open the Vocabulario del Módulo 3 study page and go through every place and feeling until you know each one by ear. (This is your best prep for the Vocabulary Check.)
2
Re-read the rule. Skim Lesson 3.03 (ser vs. estar) and Lesson 3.04 (the guided writing you already practiced) — your best prep for Lengua en acción.
3
Need the whole picture? Lessons 3.01, 3.02, 3.05, and 3.06 are all still open — revisit any of them.

One last self-test before you commit. Fill the blank aloud: “Yo ___ de los Estados Unidos, pero hoy ___ muy ocupado/a.” Then reveal.

Yo soy de los Estados Unidos, pero hoy estoy muy ocupado/a. Origin → ser (soy); a right-now feeling → estar (estoy). If that came easily, you're ready.
★ LA VIRTUD · HONESTY

A graded check is a chance to be honest — with your teacher and with yourself. Do the Vocabulary Check on your own, sound on, without peeking at notes; that's the only way the score tells you anything useful. ¡Sé honesto/a contigo mismo/a!

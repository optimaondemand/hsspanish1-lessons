🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear sentences and decide which form of ser and which subject you heard. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask: who is being talked about, and which form of ser matches?

  • Yo soy de México.yo → soy (origin)
  • ¿De dónde eres?tú → eres
  • Ella es simpática.ella → es (a trait)
  • Nosotros somos estudiantes.nosotros → somos
  • Ellos son mexicanos.ellos → son
  • ¿De dónde es usted?usted → es (respectful)

Hear the pattern? Each subject has its own form of ser. That single question — who is it, and which form? — is most of the quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · DOS ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first, then answer.

  • Mi amigo es alto e inteligente.one friend, two traits
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In «Mi amigo ___ alto e inteligente», which form of ser is correct?
✅ DECIDE · which form?
«Nosotros ___ estudiantes.» (We ___ students.)
✅ DECIDE · tú or usted?
Speaking respectfully to an elder: «¿De dónde ___ ?»

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

Match the subject to its form of ser: yo soy, tú eres, él/ella/usted es, nosotros somos, ellos/ellas/ustedes son. Use tú with friends and peers, usted to show respect.

★ LA VIRTUD · COURAGE

Two weeks ago you couldn't say your own name in Spanish. Today you introduced yourself out loud, on the record, and took a graded quiz on the verb that carries identity. That took courage — the same virtue that moved Simón Bolívar to imagine something new and say it aloud. Naming who you are, in a new language, is where every Spanish speaker begins. ¡Bien hecho!

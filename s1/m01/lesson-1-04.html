★ Aplicación — Using ser in Writing & by Ear
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned to use ser. Today you practice it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with ser in full, and it lands a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how the forms become automatic. There's no new grammar today. Instead you'll choose the right form of ser in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation. It's all ungraded practice: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to complete sentences with the correct form of ser and explain my choice.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken sentence with ser and write down what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The forms, in one breath

Before you write anything, say the five forms out loud: yo soy, tú eres, él/ella es, nosotros somos, ellos son. Hold them in your head — you're about to choose among them yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
How comfortable do you feel with the forms of ser right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You choose the form

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank with the correct form of ser (soy, eres, es, somos, son). Say why to yourself as you write. Commit to all six before you reveal the key — no peeking early; the thinking is the practice.

SER — the forms

Match the subject.
yo soy · tú eres · él/ella/usted es · nosotros somos · ellos/ellas/ustedes son

SER — its jobs

Identity, origin, and lasting traits.
Soy estudiante · Soy de México · Es inteligente
  1. Yo ______ estudiante.
  2. Nosotros ______ de México.
  3. Mi amiga Ana ______ muy amable.
  4. Tú ______ inteligente.
  5. Ellos ______ mexicanos.
  6. ¿De dónde ______ usted?
1. soy — "yo" → soy. (Yo soy estudiante.)
2. somos — "nosotros" → somos. (Nosotros somos de México.)
3. es — "ella" → es. (Mi amiga Ana es muy amable.)
4. eres — "tú" → eres. (Tú eres inteligente.)
5. son — "ellos" → son. (Ellos son mexicanos.)
6. es — "usted" takes the same form as él/ella → es. (¿De dónde es usted?)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two more sentences of your own about your real life — one that says who you are and one that says where you're from, both with ser. Read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same ser sentences you met earlier — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your accent marks, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Yo soy de México. (I am from Mexico.) — ser for origin.
2
Write what you hear…
¿De dónde eres? (Where are you from?) — note the opening ¿ and the accent on dónde.
3
Write what you hear…
Ella es simpática. (She is nice.) — es for "she"; simpática agrees with ella.
4
Write what you hear…
Nosotros somos estudiantes. (We are students.) — somos for "we."
5
Write what you hear…
Ellos son mexicanos. (They are Mexican.) — son for "they"; mexicanos agrees with the group.

Notice every sentence used ser — because each one is about identity, origin, or a lasting trait. That's the pattern your ear is learning to expect.

🧠 PART 4 · ¡A ELEGIR! 8 min

Two to finish on

Same question every time: which subject is doing the "being," and which form of ser matches it?

✅ TRY IT · choose the right form
"Tú ______ mi amigo." (You ______ my friend.)
✅ TRY IT · choose the right form
"Mi profesora ______ de Colombia." (My teacher ______ from Colombia.)

One to build yourself: how would you say "They are hard-working"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Ellos son trabajadores. "They" → son; and trabajador adds endings to agree with the group.

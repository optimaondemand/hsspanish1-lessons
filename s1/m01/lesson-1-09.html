🇲🇽 CULTURA · MÉXICO EN NORTEAMÉRICA
Un vecino, dos idiomas

México shares a long border with the United States, and Spanish is spoken by tens of millions of people within the U.S. as well — in cities like Los Angeles, Houston, and Miami. That's why learning Spanish isn't a trip to a far-off place; for many families it's the language of the neighborhood. México's capital sits on the site of ancient Tenochtitlán, layering Aztec history under a modern megacity of nearly nine million people.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

En el mercado — your semester VR scenario

Later this semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, and enter a Latin-American mercado (market) and café. You'll move between stalls, greet vendors, ask what things cost, order something, and describe what you see — all in Spanish. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so that when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE SCENARIO
  1. Saludar — greet the vendor. Choose the right register: tú with someone your age, usted with an older shopkeeper.
  2. Preguntar el precio — ask the price. ¿Cuánto cuesta? / ¿Cuánto cuestan? (you'll learn these in a later module).
  3. Pedir — order or ask for something. Quiero… / Me gustaría…
  4. Describir — describe using what you own now: ser (who or what something is). Later modules add estar, tener, and gustar.

The register choice: tú or usted?

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — think it through
You approach an older vendor at his fruit stall and want to ask where he's from. How do you greet him?

(In Latin America, ustedes is "you all" for any group — friends or strangers alike. Vosotros, Spain's informal "you all," you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

Rehearse one line before you ever step in

A vendor asks who you are. How would you introduce yourself — your name and your nationality — using ser? Say it aloud, then reveal.

Me llamo ___ y soy estadounidense. (Or your own nationality.) Soy = who I am / where I'm from → ser. This is exactly the kind of self-introduction tomorrow's quiz and the VR market both ask for.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick "market script" for yourself: write one line for each step — a greeting (with usted), a self-introduction with ser, and one description of the vendor or a product using ser (El vendedor es muy amable). Keep it; you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready.
★ LA VIRTUD · COURAGE

Walking up to a stranger in a market and speaking a language you're still learning takes courage — and a warm «Buenos días» before you ask anything makes it easier for both of you. When you enter the VR mercado, greet first, then introduce yourself: «Me llamo…, soy…». Every brave first word is how a speaker is made.

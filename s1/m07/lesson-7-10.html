🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear describing phrases and pick the right form. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask: which family is this, and who does the ending agree with?

  • alto · alta · altos · altas-o/-a → four forms
  • él es fuerte · ella es fuerte-e → no gender change
  • trabajador · trabajadora-or → adds -a for feminine
  • altos · fuertes · trabajadores · jóvenesplurals · note jóvenes
  • una casa bonitaadjective after the noun
  • Ella es morena, alta e inteligente.it all comes together

Hear the pattern? The ending always answers to the person or thing — and the describing word sits after the noun. That single habit is the whole quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · DOS ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first — a full description with several adjectives at once.

  • Ella es morena, alta e inteligente.two adjectives agreeing with one girl
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In «Ella es una chica ___ y graciosa», which form is correct?
✅ DECIDE · which form agrees?
«Mis amigos son ___.» (My friends — a mixed group — are young.)
✅ DECIDE · placement
Which is the natural Spanish order for “a generous woman”?

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

The ending agrees; the word comes after. -o/-a → four forms; -e/consonant → two forms; -or → adds -a. Plurals add -s (vowel) or -es (consonant), and joven → jóvenes. Describe with ser.

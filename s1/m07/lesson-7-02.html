📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday: how people look. Today: what they're like — plus the first gear of the grammar engine.
First you'll pick up a set of personality words (funny, shy, brave, generous…). Then you'll meet the heart of this module's grammar: an adjective ending in -o/-a has four forms, and it changes to agree with the person or thing it describes. We learn just this one pattern today — the -o → -a → -os → -as family. The rest of the adjective system (the words that don't follow this pattern) waits until tomorrow. One gear at a time.

🧠 PART 3 · LOS ADJETIVOS EN -O/-A 13 min

One adjective, four forms

In English, “tall” never changes: a tall boy, a tall girl, tall boys, tall girls. In Spanish, an adjective that ends in -o shape-shifts to match two things about the noun: its gender (masculine / feminine) and its number (singular / plural). That gives it four forms. Click the 🔊 to hear all four of alto, then say them back.

 MasculineFeminineListen
Singularalto (un chico alto)alta (una chica alta) alto · alta · altos · altas
Pluralaltos (unos chicos altos)altas (unas chicas altas)

The rule in one line: -o for one male, -a for one female, -os for a male (or mixed) group, -as for an all-female group. Describing words go with the verb ser: Él es alto. Ella es alta. Ellos son altos.

(A mixed group — even one boy among many girls — takes the masculine plural -os. That's just how Spanish works; it isn't a comment on the group.)

✅ PART 4 · A PRACTICAR 10 min

Make it agree

Pick the form that matches the person. All four options are real forms of the same adjective — you're choosing which one fits.

✅ TRY IT · which form?
“María es muy ___.” (María is very generous.)
✅ TRY IT · which form?
“Mis hermanos son ___.” (My brothers are funny.)
✅ TRY IT · which form?
“Ana y Lucía son ___.” (Ana and Lucía — two girls — are shy.)

Your turn: how would you say “He is serious”? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Él es serio. One male → the -o form; and the verb is ser (es), the verb for describing who someone is. A girl would be Ella es seria.
🌎 CULTURA · SER, NO ESTAR
Personality rides on ser

Remember Módulo 3? Estar was for how you feel right now (Estoy cansado). Ser is for who you are — your lasting traits. So personality and appearance almost always use ser: Ella es valiente (she's a brave person), not “está valiente.” The adjective system you're learning this week is the natural partner of ser.

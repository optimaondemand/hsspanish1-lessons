🌎 CULTURA · LOS ANDES
Una espina de montañas

The Andes link Perú, Chile, Argentina, Bolivia, and Ecuador into one dramatic geography — snow peaks, high plateaus, llamas, and ancient Inca roads. Across this region the Spanish language lives alongside Indigenous languages like quechua and aymara. When you place these countries on the map, you're tracing the same mountains armies once crossed on foot for independence.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

En el mercado — your semester VR scenario

Later this semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, and enter a Latin-American mercado (market) and café. You'll greet vendors, ask what things cost, order something, and describe what and whom you see. You're not performing today. This is your preview — and describing is exactly what Módulo 7 prepared you for.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE SCENARIO
  1. Saludar — greet the vendor. Choose the register: tú with someone your age, usted with an older shopkeeper.
  2. Preguntar el precio — ask the price. ¿Cuánto cuesta?
  3. Describir — describe with agreeing adjectives: La fruta es fresca y bonita. El vendedor es alto y simpático.
  4. Pedir — order or ask for something. Quiero… / Me gustaría…

The register choice: tú or usted?

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — think it through
You approach an older vendor at her fruit stall. How do you greet her?

(In Latin America, ustedes is “you all” for any group. Vosotros, Spain's informal “you all,” you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

Rehearse one description before you ever step in

You see a friendly older vendor with a big, colorful stall. How would you describe him — “The vendor is short, but very nice”? Say it aloud, then reveal.

El vendedor es bajo, pero muy simpático. ser for lasting traits; bajo and simpático both take -o to agree with the man. This is exactly the describing the VR mercado will ask of you.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick “market script” for yourself: a greeting (with usted), a price question, and two descriptions using agreeing adjectives — one of a person, one of something for sale. Keep it; you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready.
★ LA VIRTUD · SELF-GOVERNMENT

The Andes you traced today are the mountains José de San Martín crossed to free Chile and Perú — a feat of planning and iron discipline. His deepest self-government came after victory, when he set his own power aside for the greater cause. Choosing usted to honor an elder in the market is a small act of the same self-mastery: putting courtesy and respect ahead of haste. (Remember: this San Martín is the liberator — not San Martín de Porres, the friar from Módulo 5.)

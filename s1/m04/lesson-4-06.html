📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
No new grammar today — just pulling it back out of your head, which is where real learning happens.
This is your fourth meeting with tener, and the first day of Week 2. Instead of new material, today you retrieve: an ear-quiz on family words, a short production prompt, and a spiral that puts three verbs side by side to describe a family — ser + adjectives from Módulo 1 (mi hermano es alto), estar + feelings from Módulo 3 (mi hermana está cansada), and tener from this module (tengo dos hermanos). Bringing all three together is exactly what the Day-10 quiz will ask of you.

🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · TRES VERBOS 10 min

Three verbs to describe a family

To really talk about your family, you need three verbs working together — each does a different job. Listen and read across the row:

Verb (from)Its jobExample · listen
ser (Módulo 1) what someone is like · description Mi hermano es alto. (alto)
estar (Módulo 3) how someone feels right now Mi hermana está cansada.
tener (Módulo 4) what someone has · age Yo tengo dos hermanos.

Remember from Módulo 1: adjectives agree with the person. Mi hermano es alto, but mi hermana es alta. Same with feelings: cansado / cansada.

✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser, estar, or tener?
“Mi hermano ___ alto y simpático.” (My brother ___ tall and nice.) — it's a description.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser, estar, or tener?
“Mi hermana ___ cansada hoy.” (My sister ___ tired today.) — it's a right-now feeling.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser, estar, or tener?
“Mi abuela ___ setenta años.” (My grandma ___ seventy years old.) — it's an age.
✍️ PART 4 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check your verb choice, agreement, and spelling. This is a self-test, so honesty is where the value is.

1. Say: “My sister is tall and she has fifteen years (is 15).”

Mi hermana es alta y tiene quince años. Description → es (ser) with alta to agree; age → tiene (tener).

2. Say: “My parents are nice, but today they are tired.”

Mis padres son simpáticos, pero hoy están cansados. A lasting trait → son (ser); a right-now feeling → están (estar). Note the plural possessive mis and the plural agreement.

3. Say: “We have a big family and we are happy.”

Tenemos una familia grande y estamos contentos/as. Having → tenemos (tener); a feeling → estamos (estar). All three module verbs, one short paragraph.

🌎 CULTURA · LA MARIMBA
La música que une a la familia

In Guatemala, the marimba — a large wooden xylophone played by several people at once — is the sound of family gatherings, birthdays, and town festivals. It's the national instrument, and playing it is often a family affair passed from los abuelos to los hijos. When a whole family plays together, each person tiene a part, and the music only works if everyone keeps their place.

★ LA VIRTUD · RESPONSIBILITY

Retrieval practice feels harder than re-reading — and that difficulty is the point. Every time you pull ser, estar, or tener out of your memory instead of copying it, you make the pathway stronger. Taking responsibility for your own review — doing the harder, more useful practice today — is exactly what makes Thursday's quiz feel easy. ¡Tú eres capaz!

★ Saludos y cortesía — Your First Conversation
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You have the sounds. Today you say something real: hello, how are you, my name is, thank you, goodbye.
These are the words you'd actually use in the first sixty seconds of meeting a Spanish speaker — greetings, farewells, courtesy words, and how to say your name and ask someone else's. Learn these as whole chunks (don't break them apart) and you can already hold the opening of a friendly conversation.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to greet someone, say goodbye, and use por favor, gracias, and other courtesy words.
2
I will be able to say my name and ask someone else's with me llamo and ¿cómo te llamas?
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The very first word

Almost everyone's first Spanish word is hola. Remember from yesterday: the h is silent, so it's "OH-la." Play it, say it, and let's build a whole hello from there.

📚 PART 2 · SALUDOS Y DESPEDIDAS 14 min

Hellos and goodbyes

Click each 🔊 and repeat. Spanish greetings often depend on the time of day — note which is morning, afternoon, and evening.

SALUDOS · greetings

  • holahi / hello (any time)
  • buenos díasgood morning
  • buenas tardesgood afternoon
  • buenas nochesgood evening / good night
  • ¿cómo estás?how are you? (friendly)
  • ¿qué tal?how's it going? (casual)

DESPEDIDAS · farewells

  • adiósgoodbye
  • hasta luegosee you later
  • hasta mañanasee you tomorrow
  • nos vemossee you (around)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Notice the ending you already know: hasta mañana ("see you tomorrow") is exactly how these lessons sign off — and it has the ñ from Day 3! Say each farewell out loud and picture the moment you'd use it: leaving class (hasta mañana), texting a friend (nos vemos), ending a call (adiós).
😊 PART 3 · CORTESÍA Y TU NOMBRE 13 min

Polite words & saying your name

Courtesy carries you a long way in any language. Learn these five, then the chunks for introducing yourself.

CORTESÍA · courtesy

  • por favorplease
  • graciasthank you
  • de nadayou're welcome
  • perdónsorry / excuse me
  • con permisoexcuse me (to pass by)

MI NOMBRE · introducing yourself (learn as whole chunks)

  • me llamo…my name is… (lit. "I call myself")
  • ¿cómo te llamas?what's your name?
  • mucho gustonice to meet you

✅ TRY IT · introduce yourself
To tell someone your name, you say…

Put it together out loud: greet someone in the morning, say your name, and say "nice to meet you." Say the whole thing, then reveal a model.

«¡Buenos días! Me llamo ___. Mucho gusto.» That's a complete, polite Spanish introduction — on day four. Now swap in your own name and say it once more.
🌎 CULTURA · SALUDAR CON CALOR
Greetings are warm — and expected

Across the Spanish-speaking world, greetings matter. In much of Latin America it's normal to greet with a light kiss on one cheek (between women, or a man and a woman) or a warm handshake or hug. Walking into a room and saying «¡Buenos días!» to everyone is simply good manners — skipping the greeting can feel cold. A friendly hola and a smile open almost every door.

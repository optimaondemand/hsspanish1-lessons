The Americas. Most Spanish-speaking countries are in Latin America — North, Central, and South America plus the Caribbean — which is exactly why this course takes a Latin-American-first view. Spain, in Europe, is where the language started.
🌎 CULTURA · UN IDIOMA, MUCHOS PAÍSES
One language, a whole world of cultures

The Spanish-speaking world spans four continents and countless traditions — tacos in Mexico, tango in Argentina, flamenco in Spain, salsa in the Caribbean, ancient Maya and Inca heritage in Guatemala and Peru. Learning Spanish doesn't open one door; it opens dozens. As you explore the map, you're previewing the places you'll visit — through food, music, stories, and even VR — all year long.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

Your VR campus orientation — ¡Bienvenidos!

This semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, for a friendly campus orientation: you'll explore the virtual Spanish classroom, walk up to other avatars, and practice the very first thing you learned — greeting people and introducing yourself in Spanish. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so that when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE ORIENTATION
  1. Saludar — greet someone. Walk up to another avatar and say «¡Hola! Buenos días.»
  2. Presentarte — introduce yourself. «Me llamo ___. Soy de ___.»
  3. Preguntar — ask a name. «¿Cómo te llamas?» and reply «Mucho gusto.»
  4. Ser cortés — be courteous. Use por favor, gracias, and say goodbye with «¡Hasta luego!»

Rehearse your first VR line

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — say it aloud
You meet a new classmate's avatar. Which opening feels most like you?

Rehearse a full introduction out loud, as if you were already in VR: greet, give your name, say where you're from, and sign off politely. Say it, then reveal a model.

«¡Hola! Buenos días. Me llamo ___. Soy de ___. Mucho gusto. ¡Hasta luego!» That's a complete, courteous VR introduction — and it's all Module 0 vocabulary. Say it once more so it's ready.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write yourself a short "orientation script": one greeting, your name, where you're from, one question, and a goodbye. Keep it — you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready. Courtesy travels in any language: greet first, always.

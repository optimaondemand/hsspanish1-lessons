🌎 CULTURA · SUDAMÉRICA HISPANOHABLANTE
Colombia y sus vecinos

Colombia sits at the top of South America, bordered by Venezuela (capital: Caracas), Ecuador (Quito), and Perú (Lima) — all Spanish-speaking. Its own capital, Bogotá, sits high in the Andes at about 2,600 m. Across the region, weekends mean being outdoors and together: people corren, dance, and comparten food in the plaza. The free-time verbs of this module describe daily life all across the continent.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

En el mercado — your semester VR scenario

Later this semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, and enter a Latin-American mercado (market) and café. You'll move between stalls, greet vendors, ask what things cost, order something, and talk about what you like to do. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so that when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE SCENARIO
  1. Saludar — greet the vendor. Choose the right register: tú with someone your age, usted with an older shopkeeper.
  2. Preguntar el precio — ask the price. ¿Cuánto cuesta? / ¿Cuánto cuestan?
  3. Pedir — order or ask for something. Quiero… / Me gustaría…
  4. Conversar — talk about your free time using what you own: -er / -ir verbs (como, leo, bebo, escribo) and me gusta + infinitive (me gusta correr).

The register choice: tú or usted?

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — think it through
You approach an older vendor at his fruit stall. How do you greet him?

(In Latin America, ustedes is "you all" for any group — friends or strangers alike. Vosotros, Spain's informal "you all," you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

Rehearse one line before you ever step in

At the café stall you chat with the owner about your afternoons. How would you say "In the café I read and I drink coffee" — using two -er verbs? Say it aloud, then reveal.

En el café leo y bebo café. leer and beber are both -er verbs; yo → -o. These are exactly the forms tomorrow's quiz asks for.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick "market script" for yourself: write one line for each step — a greeting (with usted), a price question, an order, and one line about your free time using an -er / -ir verb. Keep it; you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready.
★ LA VIRTUD · HONESTY

A market runs on honesty — a fair price, an honest answer about what's fresh, a straight deal on both sides. When you enter the VR mercado, deal plainly: ask the real price, say what you truly want, thank the vendor. Honesty isn't only for big moments like Bartolomé de las Casas defending the truth — it's also the small daily fairness that keeps a marketplace, and a community, working.

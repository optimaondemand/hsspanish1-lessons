Lesson 6.03
★ El presente -er / -ir — Almost Identical Twins
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Here's the shortcut that makes two verb families feel like one: -er and -ir verbs are identical except in one form.
Yesterday you met comer (-er) and vivir (-ir) separately. Today you lay them side by side and the pattern jumps out: yo, tú, él/ella, and ellos use the same endings for both families. The only difference is the nosotros form: -er uses -emos (comemos), -ir uses -imos (vivimos). Learn that one difference and you've learned two whole families.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to conjugate regular -er and -ir verbs in the present tense.
2
I will be able to explain the one form where -er and -ir differ (nosotros: -emos vs. -imos).
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 8 min

A quick look back

You already own both verbs from yesterday. Say them aloud: Yo como (I eat), Yo vivo (I live). Now say the nosotros forms back to back: comemos … vivimos. Hear that -e- vs. -i-? That's the whole difference between the two families.

🧠 PART 4 · LA ÚNICA DIFERENCIA 10 min

The one rule that matters

-ER — nosotros ends in -emos

como · comes · come · comemos · comen

Nosotros comemos juntos. (We eat together.)
Nosotros leemos mucho. (We read a lot.)

-IR — nosotros ends in -imos

vivo · vives · vive · vivimos · viven

Nosotros vivimos aquí. (We live here.)
Nosotros escribimos cartas. (We write letters.)

The shortcut: conjugate any -er or -ir verb with the shared endings — then, for nosotros only, ask: does the infinitive end in -er (→ -emos) or -ir (→ -imos)?

✅ TRY IT · -emos or -imos?
"Nosotros ___ en Colombia." (from vivir — We live in Colombia.)
✅ TRY IT · -emos or -imos?
"Nosotros ___ en el parque." (from correr — We run in the park.)

Your turn: how would you say "We write" (from escribir)? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Nosotros escribimos. escribir is an -ir verb → the nosotros ending is -imos. (Compare: comemos from the -er verb comer.)
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

«En mi tiempo libre, yo corro en el parque y leo muchos libros. Mi hermano escribe canciones. Los fines de semana, nosotros comemos juntos y compartimos historias. ¡Yo vivo feliz!»

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Point to each verb and name its family: corro, leo, escribe, comemos are -er; compartimos, vivo are -ir. Which two are nosotros forms? (comemos — -emos; compartimos — -imos.) That's the one difference, live in a sentence.
🌎 CULTURA · COLOMBIA
Un país de tiempo libre bien vivido

In Colombia, Sundays bring the Ciclovía: cities like Bogotá close major avenues to cars so families can correr, bike, and walk together. Los colombianos viven el tiempo libre en la calle — free time is something you do with people, out in the open. Notice the verbs of this module (correr, compartir, vivir) describing a real weekend.

★ LA VIRTUD · HONESTY

When a practice item feels shaky, honesty with yourself is what turns it into learning. Don't tell yourself «I get it» and move on — say out loud which form you're unsure of (is it comemos or comimos?), then check. Naming the gap honestly is exactly how you close it.

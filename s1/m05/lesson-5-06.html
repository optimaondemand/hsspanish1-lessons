📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
No new grammar today — just pulling it back out of your head, which is where real learning happens.
This is your fourth meeting with gustar, and the first day of Week 2. Instead of new material, today you retrieve: an ear-quiz on the food words, a short production prompt, and a spiral that reconnects gustar to what you already own — talking about school with -ar verbs (Módulo 2), family with tener (Módulo 4), and food with ser/estar (Módulos 1 & 3). Bringing it all together is exactly what the Day-10 quiz will ask.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…

I will be able to recognize spoken food words and match them to meaning.
2
I will be able to combine gustar with earlier tools — -ar verbs, tener, and ser/estar.

🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · TODO JUNTO 10 min

Four tools, one conversation about food

You now have several tools, and they team up naturally when you talk about food. Listen and read across each row:

Tool (from)Its jobExample · listen
gustar (Módulo 5) what you like Me gusta el café.
-ar verb (Módulo 2) an action at school Yo estudio español.
tener (Módulo 4) family · what you have Yo tengo dos hermanos.
ser / estar (Módulos 1 & 3) what food is · how it is now El café es de Colombia; está caliente.

Notice that last row: es de Colombia tells you where the coffee is from (origin → ser), and está caliente tells you its condition right now (→ estar). Same drink, two verbs — just like Módulo 3.

✅ SPIRAL CHECK · which tool?
“A mí me ___ la clase de español.” (I like Spanish class.) — it's a preference.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser or estar?
“El café ___ de Costa Rica.” (The coffee is from Costa Rica.) — it's origin.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · ser or estar?
“El café ___ caliente.” (The coffee is hot.) — it's a condition right now.
✍️ PART 4 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check. This is a self-test, so being honest with yourself is where the value is.

1. Say: “I like Spanish class and I study a lot.”

Me gusta la clase de español y estudio mucho. A preference → gusta (gustar); an action → estudio (an -ar verb from M2).

2. Say: “My sister likes fruit, and she has two brothers.”

A mi hermana le gusta la fruta, y tiene dos hermanos. A preference → le gusta (gustar); family/possession → tiene (tener, from M4).

3. Say: “The coffee is from Costa Rica and it's hot.”

El café es de Costa Rica y está caliente. Origin → es (ser); condition right now → está (estar) — the M3 contrast, now about food.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER OTRA VEZ 5 min

Valeria returns — listen and read

You met this passage on Day 5. Press play, follow along, and this time notice how gusta, gustan, and encanta each do their job.

Play the reading

«Hola, me llamo Valeria. Soy de Costa Rica. En mi país, el desayuno favorito es el gallo pinto — arroz con frijoles. ¡A mí me encanta! También me gusta el café; el café de Costa Rica es muy famoso. A mi hermana le gustan las frutas frescas, pero no le gustan las verduras. En mi casa nos gusta cocinar juntos los domingos. ¿Y a ti? ¿Qué te gusta?»

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Point to each preference verb and say its job: me encanta (love, one thing), me gusta (like, one thing), le gustan (like, several), nos gusta cocinar (like an action). Could you add one sentence about Valeria using tener? (Hint: Valeria tiene una hermana.)
🌎 CULTURA · EL ALMUERZO
El casado costarricense

In Costa Rica the classic lunch is the casado — a single plate with rice, beans, a protein like pollo, sweet plantain, and salad. In much of Latin America el almuerzo is the biggest meal of the day, often shared with family. When someone serves you one, the courteous reply is «¡Gracias, se ve delicioso!» (Thanks, it looks delicious!).

★ LA VIRTUD · COURTESY

Retrieval practice feels harder than re-reading — and that difficulty is the point. But there's a courtesy in it too: every time you pull gustar, tener, or ser/estar out of memory now, you're preparing to hold a real conversation and truly listen to what someone else likes. Tú eres capaz, y hoy estás un paso más cerca del examen.

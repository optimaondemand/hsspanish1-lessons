Lesson 5.03
★ Me gusta… — The “Backwards” Verb
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Here's the whole module in one sentence: Me gusta el café. Today you learn exactly how it works.
Yesterday you met the pronouns me, te, le, nos, les. Today they snap onto gustar. The surprise: Spanish doesn't literally say “I like coffee.” It says “Coffee is pleasing to me” — the thing you like is the star of the sentence, and you are along for the ride. That's why we call gustar “backwards.” Once you see it, one more rule follows: use gusta for one thing (or an action) and gustan for several things.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to say what people like using me/te/le/nos/les + gusta.
2
I will be able to choose gusta (one thing / an action) vs. gustan (several things).

📚 PART 2 · GUSTAR CON PRONOMBRES 10 min

Listen and repeat — the pronoun does the work

The verb barely changes — it stays gusta. What changes is the pronoun in front, and that's what tells us who likes it. Click each 🔊 and repeat the sentence aloud.

WhoSentenceListen
me (to me)Me gusta el café. (I like coffee.)
te (to you)¿Te gusta el arroz? (Do you like rice?)
le (to him/her)Le gusta la fruta. (He/She likes fruit.)
nos (to us)Nos gusta cocinar. (We like to cook.)

See it? Nos gusta cocinar — when the thing liked is an action (an infinitive like cocinar), you still use gusta.

🧠 PART 3 · ¿GUSTA O GUSTAN? 8 min

The one rule that matters

Because the thing liked is really the subject, the verb agrees with it, not with you:

GUSTA — one thing or an action

Singular noun or an infinitive.

Me gusta el café. (one thing)
Nos gusta cocinar. (an action)
¿Te gusta la leche?

GUSTAN — several things

Plural noun → add the -n.

Me gustan las verduras.
Le gustan los frijoles.
Nos gustan las frutas.

The shortcut: one thing (or an action) → gusta. More than one thing → gustan. The pronoun (me/te/le/nos/les) never changes for singular vs. plural — only the verb does.

Want to say you love it, not just like it? Swap in encantar — it works exactly the same way: Me encanta el pollo.

🌎 CULTURA · COSTA RICA
«¡Pura vida!»

In Costa Rica, our featured country, the phrase you'll hear everywhere is «pura vida» — literally “pure life,” used for hello, goodbye, thanks, and “all good.” Ask a tico (a Costa Rican) «¿Te gusta el café?» and you'll likely get a warm “¡Claro, pura vida!” Costa Rican coffee is famous worldwide — a perfect first thing to say me gusta about.

★ LA VIRTUD · COURTESY

The most courteous question in any language is the one that asks about the other person: «¿Y a ti? ¿Qué te gusta?» (And you? What do you like?) Learning gustar isn't only about announcing your own tastes — it's about being able to ask, and truly listen for, someone else's.

Lesson 8.03
★ Mi autorretrato — Build a Full Self-Portrait
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A model first, then your own. You'll read Lucía's self-portrait — all four systems in one paragraph — then draft yours.
Yesterday you lined up the four verbs. Today you see them do real work. Lucía introduces herself the way a whole person does: who she is, how she feels, her family and age, what she looks like, what she loves. Read her, listen to her, then borrow her structure to build your portrait. This draft is your foundation for the graded Tarea oral on Day 8.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to read a full self-portrait and identify where each of the four systems is used.
2
I will be able to draft my own five-line self-portrait using ser, estar, tener, and gustar.
⏰ PART 1 · ANTES DE LEER 6 min

Before you read

A good self-portrait moves in a natural order: name & origin (ser), age & family (tener), looks (ser/tener), feelings (estar), likes (gustar). Watch for that arc as you read Lucía.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
If you had to open your own portrait with one sentence, which would you pick?
📚 PART 2 · EL PERFIL DE LUCÍA 15 min

Listen first, then read

Click 🔊 to hear Lucía's whole self-portrait read by a native speaker. Listen once with your eyes closed, then read along a second time.

Escuchar el autorretrato completo — Lucía

«Hola, me llamo Lucía. Soy de Guadalajara, México, y soy estudiante. Tengo quince años y tengo una hermana menor. Soy baja y tengo el pelo largo y moreno. Normalmente soy muy alegre, pero hoy estoy un poco nerviosa porque tengo un examen. Me gusta la música y me gustan los libros. ¡Así soy yo!»

Not sure of a word? Reveal a mini-glossary — but try to read without it first.

me llamo = my name is · estudiante = student · quince años = fifteen years old · hermana menor = younger sister · baja = short · el pelo largo y moreno = long dark hair · normalmente = usually · alegre = cheerful · hoy = today · un poco = a little · porque = because · un examen = a test · así soy yo = that's me
✅ PART 3 · ¿COMPRENDISTE? 9 min

Check your understanding

✅ FROM THE READING · which system?
Lucía says «Tengo quince años». Which system is that, and what does it mean?
✅ FROM THE READING
Why does she say «hoy estoy nerviosa» with estar, not ser?
✅ FROM THE READING
She says «me gusta la música» but «me gustan los libros». Why the change to gustan?
✍️ PART 4 · TU AUTORRETRATO 10 min

Now build yours

Say (or jot) one true line for each prompt, out loud, in Lucía's order. Keep this draft — you'll speak it for the graded Tarea oral on Day 8.

LineSystemSentence frame
1 · Name & originserMe llamo ___ y soy de ___.
2 · Age & familytenerTengo ___ años y tengo ___.
3 · Looksser / tenerSoy ___ y tengo el pelo ___.
4 · Feeling todayestarHoy estoy ___.
5 · LikesgustarMe gusta(n) ___.

Stuck on line 3 (looks)? Say it aloud, then reveal a model you can adapt.

Soy alto/a y tengo el pelo corto y rubio. — ser for the overall trait (tall), tener for the feature (hair). Swap in whatever is true for you.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Record your five lines on your phone and play them back. Do they flow like Lucía's? This is the exact task you'll submit on Day 8 — a head start now makes that day easy.
★ LA VIRTUD · HONESTY

A self-portrait is only worth making if it's true. Lucía admits she's nerviosa hoy — she doesn't pretend. Honesty is the semester's virtue carried by Bartolomé de las Casas, who spoke the uncomfortable truth in defense of Indigenous peoples. Describe yourself as you really are; the language sounds better when it's honest.

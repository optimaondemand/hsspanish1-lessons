⏰ PART 1 · PREPARACIÓN 10 min

Rehearse your lines before you put on the headset

In the mercado you'll greet an older vendor, so choose the courteous usted register. Listen, then rehearse each move aloud.

★ LA VIRTUD · COURTESY

Greeting the vendor before you ask a price isn't just grammar — it's courtesy, the virtue of San Martín de Porres, the humble Peruvian friar who treated everyone with kindness. In Spanish-speaking markets a warm «Buenos días» comes first; rushing to business can seem cold. Courtesy travels in any language — and in any world, real or virtual.

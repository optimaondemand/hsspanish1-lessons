Lesson 8.04
★ Describir a otros — and Make the Adjectives Agree
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday: describe yourself. Today: describe someone else — and make every adjective agree.
Talking about another person means switching to the he/she forms — es, está, tiene, le gusta — and it puts pressure on adjective agreement from Módulo 7: a boy is alto, a girl is alta; a group is altos. You'll meet Carlos, review agreement, and practice describing a third person cleanly. No new grammar — just precision.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to describe another person using the he/she forms of all four systems.
2
I will be able to make adjectives agree in gender and number with the person I'm describing.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Switching to “he/she”

To describe another person, each verb shifts one form: soy→es, estoy→está, tengo→tiene, me gusta→le gusta. Listen once to anchor the shift.

  • Descripción (M7)how we describe a person — from Módulo 7
  • Concordancia -o / -aadjective agreement — the -o/-a switch
💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Whom will you describe on the Day-8 recording, besides yourself?
📚 PART 2 · CARLOS DESCRIBE A SU HERMANO 13 min

Listen first, then read

Carlos describes another person — notice every verb is in the he/she form and every adjective agrees. Click 🔊, listen twice, then read.

Escuchar la descripción completa — Carlos

«Mi mejor amigo es Carlos. Es de Colombia y tiene dieciséis años. Es alto y tiene el pelo corto. Es muy simpático y trabajador. Hoy está un poco cansado porque tiene mucha tarea. A él le gusta el fútbol y le gustan los videojuegos.»

Reveal a mini-glossary if you need it — try to read without it first.

mi mejor amigo = my best friend · dieciséis = sixteen · alto = tall · el pelo corto = short hair · simpático = nice · trabajador = hard-working · cansado = tired · mucha tarea = a lot of homework · a él le gusta = he likes · el fútbol = soccer · los videojuegos = video games

✍️ PART 4 · DESCRIBE A UNA PERSONA 10 min

Your turn — describe someone real

Using Carlos's shape, describe the person you chose in Part 1. Say one line per system, out loud, matching every adjective to their gender.

SystemFrame
ser (who)Mi ___ es ___ y es de ___.
tener (age/features)Tiene ___ años y tiene el pelo ___.
ser (personality)Es ___ y ___.
estar (feeling)Hoy está ___.
gustar (likes)Le gusta(n) ___.

Quick check: how do you say «She is nice and she has green eyes»? Say it, then reveal.

Ella es simpática y tiene los ojos verdes. simpática agrees with a female; tiene is the he/she form of tener; los ojos verdes — the color follows the noun.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Describe two people at once (a mixed pair) and watch the plural agreement kick in: Mis amigos son altos y simpáticos; les gusta el fútbol. Notice les gusta for “they like.”
★ LA VIRTUD · SERVICE

Describing someone accurately — really seeing them — is a quiet form of service: you give them your attention. The semester's figure for service is Roberto Clemente, the Puerto Rican baseball star who died delivering aid to earthquake victims. Clemente es un héroe; tiene un lugar en el Salón de la Fama. — ser and tener, describing a life of service.

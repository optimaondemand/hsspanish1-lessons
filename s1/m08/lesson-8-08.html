📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
The whole self-portrait, out loud and on the record — your capstone speaking task.
First a quick pronunciation refresh (pure vowels, stress, the ñ), then your graded Tarea oral: record a complete self-portrait — origin, age, a physical and personality description, what you like, and how you feel right now. This is the same portrait you drafted on Day 3, now spoken with care. Same fixed three-criteria rubric you've used all semester.

🔈 PART 1 · REPASO DE FONÉTICA 10 min

Three habits of a good accent

HabitRulePractice word
Pure vowelsa-e-i-o-u are short and never glidefamilia, ojos
Stressends in vowel/-n/-s → 2nd-to-last; a tilde overridescon-TEN-to, es-TÁ
The ñthe «ny» sound, as in «canyon»años → A-nyos, español

Notice that años (years) — a word you'll say in your age line — needs the ñ. Rehearse the integrated models aloud, matching the speaker's vowels and stress:

  • Modelo 1«Soy de México, tengo quince años y estoy contento.»
  • Modelo 3«Somos estudiantes; estamos en la escuela y nos gusta aprender.»
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Record yourself on your phone saying the vowel chain a – e – i – o – u and then the word años five times, and play it back. Matching a native speaker's short, steady vowels and a clean ñ is the single fastest way to sharpen your accent before you record the graded task.

Lucía's portrait — the shape yours should follow

Replay Lucía from Day 3. She hits every piece your recording needs: origin, age & family, looks, feeling today, likes.

💡 CONSEJO · plan before you record

If it helps you, write out your sentences first and read them aloud a couple of times before you record. Many students sound calmer and clearer once they’ve drafted a quick script — reading from your notes is completely fine. Just aim to sound natural, not rushed.

Record directly in Canvas (or upload an audio/video file). 15 points.

How it's scored — the rubric

Three criteria, 5 points each, 15 total. Read it before you record.

CriterionWhat earns 5 / 5Pts

✅ SELF-REVIEW · check these before you submit
  • Did I include all five pieces (origin, age, looks + personality, likes, feeling)?
  • Did I use the right verb for each — ser, tener, ser/tener, gustar, estar?
  • Do my -o/-a adjectives match me (cansado vs. cansada)?
  • Did I say años with a real ñ, and keep my vowels short?
  • Did I play it back once and re-record if needed before submitting?
★ LA VIRTUD · COURAGE

Recording a full paragraph out loud takes courage — the virtue of Simón Bolívar, who dared to imagine a free continent. Your first take will feel awkward; that's the sign you're doing it, not doing it wrong. Rehearse, breathe, and press record. ¡Tú puedes!

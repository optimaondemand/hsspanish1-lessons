Lesson 8.05
★ Dos perfiles y las siete virtudes del semestre
Lesson · about 40 minutes · end of Week 1
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
First we read both profiles side by side. Then we gather the whole semester's virtues — seven of them, seven people — into one picture.
You've built a self-portrait (Lucía) and a portrait of another (Carlos). Today you read them together to feel how the same four systems describe any person. Then comes the heart of the module: a synthesis of the seven virtues you met across the semester — each carried by a real figure from the Spanish-speaking world. Describing a person, it turns out, is also a way of naming what they stand for.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to compare two self/other portraits and track all four systems across both.
2
I will be able to name the semester's seven virtues and the figure connected to each.
📚 PART 1 · LOS DOS PERFILES 12 min

Read them together

Replay both, one after the other. Lucía describes herself (yo forms); Carlos describes another (he/she forms). Same four systems, different endings.

  • Lucía (yo)self-portrait — soy / tengo / estoy / me gusta
  • Carlos (él)describing another — es / tiene / está / le gusta
✅ ACROSS BOTH · spot the difference
Lucía says me gusta; Carlos's friend gets le gusta. What changed?
✅ ACROSS BOTH
Both say a feeling with estoy / está and add hoy (today). Why estar?
★ PART 2 · EL HILO DE LAS VIRTUDES 16 min

Seven virtues, seven lives — the whole semester in one table

Each module carried one of Optima's virtues, embodied by a real person from across the Spanish-speaking world. Here they are together. Read each one and say the little Spanish line aloud — every line uses the systems you now own.

VirtueFigure · countryWhy · una línea en español
Courage
(valentía)		Simón Bolívar · VenezuelaLed the fight to free much of South America. Bolívar es valiente; tiene el nombre de «el Libertador».
Service
(servicio)		Roberto Clemente · Puerto RicoBaseball great who died delivering earthquake aid. Es un héroe; le gusta ayudar a los demás.

(perseverancia)		Frida Kahlo · MéxicoPainted through a lifetime of pain. Es pintora; sus cuadros están en museos del mundo.
Responsibility
(responsabilidad)		Rigoberta Menchú · GuatemalaDefended Indigenous rights; Nobel Peace laureate. Es activista y tiene el Premio Nobel de la Paz.
Courtesy
(cortesía)		San Martín de Porres · PerúHumble friar who cared for the poor and sick. Es amable con todos; está siempre al servicio de otros.
Honesty
(honestidad)		Bartolomé de las CasasSpoke the hard truth to defend Indigenous peoples. Es honesto; tiene el valor de decir la verdad.
Self-government
(autogobierno)		José de San Martín · ArgentinaFreed three nations, then stepped away from power. Es disciplinado; no está interesado en el poder.

Pick one figure whose virtue you'd like to carry into next semester. Say a full sentence about them — then reveal a model.

Frida Kahlo es una pintora mexicana; tiene mucha perseverancia y sus cuadros están en museos famosos. — three systems (ser, tener, estar) describing a life. You can do this for any of the seven.

★ LA VIRTUD · LAS SIETE, JUNTAS

Courage, service, perseverance, responsibility, courtesy, honesty, self-government — seven ways of being a good person, each shown by a real life from Venezuela to Argentina to Peru. You've spent a semester learning to describe people in Spanish. The deeper lesson is which qualities are worth describing. Carry one of these seven into Semester 2.

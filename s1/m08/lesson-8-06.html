Lesson 8.06
★ Repaso general — Spiral Across the Whole Semester
Lesson · about 40 minutes · Week 2 begins
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One relaxed, comprehensive run through everything — ear first, then your voice. This is your best preparation for the graded days ahead.
The graded assessments (Days 7–10) are cumulative — they pull from all of Semester 1. Today you spiral back through the verbs that will show up: ser (M1), estar (M3), tener (M4), gustar (M5), and adjective agreement (M7). Warm your ear with clips from each module, test yourself, and then produce a few sentences of your own. Nothing here is graded; everything here is practice.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize each of the four verbs by ear when I hear it in a sentence.
2
I will be able to produce correct sentences across all four systems on demand.
🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 12 min

One clip per system — listen and name the verb

Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say which system you heard before you read the English.

  • ser (M1)«Yo soy…» — identity
  • estar (M3)«Yo estoy en casa.» — location
  • tener (M4)«Yo tengo…» — age / family
  • gustar (M5)«Me gusta…» — likes
  • descripción (M7)describing looks & personality

Now hear all three systems stacked in one sentence — the capstone move:

  • todo junto«Soy de México, tengo quince años y estoy contento.»
🧠 PART 2 · REPASO EN ESPIRAL 14 min

Mixed practice — every system in play

✅ TRY IT · from M1
«¿De dónde ___ tú?» (Where are you from?)
✅ TRY IT · from M4
«Mi abuela ___ setenta años.» (My grandmother is seventy.)
✅ TRY IT · from M5
«A nosotros ___ las películas.» (We like movies.)
✅ TRY IT · from M3
«Los estudiantes ___ nerviosos hoy.» (The students are nervous today.)
✅ TRY IT · from M7
«Mis hermanas son ___.» (My sisters are tall.)
✍️ PART 3 · PRODUCE 10 min

Say it yourself — no options this time

Speak a full sentence for each prompt, out loud. Then reveal one correct version to compare.

1. Say your name and where you're from (ser).

Me llamo ___ y soy de ___.

2. Say your age and one family member (tener).

Tengo ___ años y tengo un hermano / una hermana.

3. Say how you feel right now (estar) and one thing you like (gustar).

Hoy estoy ___. Me gusta(n) ___. Remember: gusta for one thing, gustan for several.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Run the full Vocabulario del Módulo 8 flip-cards once with the English covered, then string five words into one paragraph about yourself. That paragraph is essentially your Day-8 recording.
★ LA VIRTUD · PERSEVERANCE

A whole semester of Spanish comes back today in one review — and you can feel how much has stuck. That's not luck; it's the reward of perseverance, the virtue of Frida Kahlo, who kept working through every setback. Steady practice, day after day, is exactly what carries you through the gate ahead. ¡Sigue adelante!

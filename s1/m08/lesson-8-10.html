🔒 WHAT “GATE” MEANS — READ THIS FIRST

This quiz is the gate to Semester 2. To move on, aim for 80% or higher (24 / 30). You get 2 attempts, and your higher score counts — so you have real room to reach the bar.

Below 80% is not a course failure and not the end of the road. If both attempts land under the bar, you'll get a targeted review plan — a short, focused set of the exact skills to revisit (usually one or two of the four systems) and a chance to show mastery before starting Semester 2. The gate exists to make sure the foundation is solid, because everything in Semester 2 builds on these four verbs.

🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the gate

Part of the quiz is listening. Warm up: click each 🔊, listen twice, and name the system before reading the English.

  • «Yo soy…»identity → ser
  • «Yo estoy en casa.»location → estar
  • «Yo tengo…»age / family → tener
  • «Me gusta…»likes → gustar
  • Tres sistemas juntos«Mi hermana es alta, tiene el pelo largo y le gusta la música.»

Hear it? Each system has a signature: soy/es (identity), estoy/está (feeling/place), tengo/tiene (age/family/features), me/le gusta (likes). That's the whole quiz.

✅ DECIDE · which system?

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

ser = who/what you are · estar = where you are + how you feel right now · tener = what you have (age, family, features) · gustar = what pleases you (gusta = one thing, gustan = several).

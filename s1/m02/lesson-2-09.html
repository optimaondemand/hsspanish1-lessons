🌎 CULTURA · LAS ANTILLAS
Tres islas, una región

Puerto Rico, Cuba y la República Dominicana son las tres islas hispanohablantes del Caribe (las Antillas Mayores). Puerto Rico es un territorio de los Estados Unidos, pero el español es el idioma de la vida diaria — en casa, en la escuela y en la calle. Across the region, Spanish mixes with African and Taíno roots — you hear it in reggaetón, salsa, and bomba.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

En el mercado — your semester VR scenario

Later this semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, and enter a Latin-American mercado (market) and café. You'll move between stalls, greet vendors, ask what things cost, order something, and describe what you see — all in Spanish. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so that when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE SCENARIO
  1. Saludar — greet the vendor. Choose the right register: tú with someone your age, usted with an older shopkeeper.
  2. Preguntar el precio — ask the price. ¿Cuánto cuesta? / ¿Cuánto cuestan?
  3. Pedir — order or ask for something. Quiero… / Necesito… (there's an -ar verb you know — necesitar).
  4. Describir — describe using what you own: ser (who/what it is) and your -ar verbs (what people do — trabajar, hablar, usar).

The register choice: tú or usted?

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — think it through
You approach an older vendor at his fruit stall. How do you greet him?

(In Latin America, ustedes is "you all" for any group — friends or strangers alike. Vosotros, Spain's informal "you all," you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

Rehearse one line before you ever step in

You chat with the vendor about his work. How would you say "You work here and you speak two languages" (usted) — using two -ar verbs? Say it aloud, then reveal.

Usted trabaja aquí y habla dos idiomas. Two actions → two -ar verbs; the usted form takes the same ending as él/ella — -a (trabaja, habla). This is exactly the conjugation the quiz asks for.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick "market script" for yourself: write one line for each step — a greeting (with usted), a price question, an order using necesitar, and one description using ser and one using an -ar verb. Keep it; you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready.
★ LA VIRTUD · COURTESY

Choosing usted for an older vendor isn't just grammar — it's courtesy. In Spanish-speaking markets, a warm «Buenos días» before you ask a price is expected; skipping straight to business can seem cold. When you enter the VR mercado, greet first. Courtesy travels in any language.

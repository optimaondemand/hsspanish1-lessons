★ El presente regular — One Pattern, Every -ar Verb
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you conjugated one verb. Today the big idea clicks: those five endings work for every regular -ar verb.
This is the engine of the whole module. Learn the recipe once — drop the -ar, add the ending — and you can say hablo, trabajas, escucha, practicamos, usan and dozens more. You'll also draw one careful line: an -ar verb tells what someone does; ser (from Módulo 1) tells who someone is. Keeping those two jobs apart is today's second goal.

🧠 PART 2 · LA RECETA 10 min

The recipe: drop -ar, add the ending

Every regular -ar verb works in two steps. 1) Take the infinitive (hablar) and drop the -ar to get the stem (habl-). 2) Add the ending that matches the subject. Watch the same five endings ride on three different verbs:

(Note: practicar and llegar keep the same spoken endings; their spelling shifts only in forms you'll meet later. For now, every verb on your list follows this table.)

🧠 PART 3 · ACCIÓN O IDENTIDAD? 10 min

An action verb is not ser

A common beginner slip is to glue ser onto an action — saying “soy estudio” for “I study.” Don't. An -ar verb already contains the doer. Keep the two jobs separate:

SER — who someone is

Identity, role, origin (from Módulo 1).

Yo soy estudiante. (I am a student.)
Ella es maestra. (She is a teacher.)

Verbo -ar — what someone does

An action, all by itself — no ser needed.

Yo estudio español. (I study Spanish.)
Ella enseña ciencias. (She teaches science.)

The shortcut: if you're saying who someone is → ser. If you're saying what someone does → just the -ar verb — never both.

✅ TRY IT · conjugate or choose
"Mi hermano ___ en un café." (My brother ___ at a café.) — it's an action.
✅ TRY IT · conjugate or choose
"Yo ___ estudiante en Optima." (I ___ a student at Optima.) — it's identity.

Your turn: how would you say "We listen in class" (escuchar)? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Nosotros escuchamos en la clase. An action → just the -ar verb; "we" → the -amos ending: escuchamos. No ser needed.

🌎 CULTURA · PUERTO RICO
La Isla del Encanto

Puerto Rico es una isla del Caribe y un territorio de los Estados Unidos — but Spanish es the language of everyday life. Its capital, San Juan, mixes 500-year-old Spanish forts with modern schools. Puerto Ricans call their home «la Isla del Encanto» (the Island of Enchantment), and students there estudian, hablan, and practican in two languages every day.

★ LA VIRTUD · SERVICE

Roberto Clemente (Puerto Rico) was one of baseball's greatest players — but he is remembered just as much for how he served. He spent every off-season delivering food and aid to people in need, and in 1972 he died in a plane crash carrying earthquake relief to Nicaragua. Él es un héroe; siempre ayuda a los demás. Service means using what you have — talent, time, a plane — for others.

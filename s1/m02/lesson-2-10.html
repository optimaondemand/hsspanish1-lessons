🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear sentences and decide which form of the verb you heard. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask the one question that matters: who is doing the action — and so which ending should it have?

  • Yo estudio español.yo → -o
  • ¿Tú estudias mucho?tú → -as
  • Ella estudia en casa.ella → -a
  • Nosotros estudiamos juntos.nosotros → -amos
  • Ellos estudian ciencias.ellos → -an
  • Yo soy de México.identity → ser (not an action)

Hear the difference? The ending changed with the subject each time — -o, -as, -a, -amos, -an — while the last clip used ser because it names who she is, not what she does. That single question — which subject, or is it identity? — is the whole quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · DOS ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first, then answer from what you hear.

  • Mi maestra trabaja en la escuela.trabajar — ella/usted form
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In «Mi maestra ___ en la escuela», which form did you hear?
✅ DECIDE · which subject, which ending?
«Nosotros ___ a la maestra.» (We ___ to the teacher.) — escuchar.
✅ DECIDE · an action or identity?
«Yo ___ estudiante en Optima.» (I ___ a student at Optima.)

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

Drop the -ar, add the ending that matches the subject: -o (yo), -as (tú), -a (él/ella/usted), -amos (nosotros), -an (ellos/ellas/ustedes). And an action stands alone — use ser only for who someone is.

📝 PART 3 · LA PRUEBA DE GRAMÁTICA 18 min

Grammar Quiz — the regular -ar present

This is the graded quiz for Módulo 2. Before you launch it, read what to expect:

  • 30 points total · 2 attempts (your higher score counts).
  • Includes 10–15 points of listening — short clips like the ones you just heard. Have your sound on.
  • The rest asks you to conjugate -ar verbs for each subject and to tell an action from identity (ser).
  • No new vocabulary — everything comes from this module and your earlier work with ser.

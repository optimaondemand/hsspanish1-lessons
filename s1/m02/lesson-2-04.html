Lesson 2.04
★ Aplicación — Using the -ar Verbs
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the recipe. Today you use it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the -ar pattern, and it lands a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how conjugation becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. Instead you'll pick the right ending yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation. It's all ungraded: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to complete sentences with the correct -ar ending and explain why.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken -ar sentence and write down exactly what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The recipe, in one breath

Before you write anything, say the recipe out loud: drop the -ar, add the ending — -o, -as, -a, -amos, -an for yo, tú, él/ella/usted, nosotros, ellos/ellas/ustedes. Hold that in your head — you're about to make the calls yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
The five -ar endings — how comfortable do you feel right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You make the choice

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank with the correct present-tense form of the -ar verb in parentheses. Say why to yourself as you write (which subject → which ending). Commit to all six before you reveal the key — the thinking is the practice.

The recipe

Drop -ar, add the ending.
-o · -as · -a · -amos · -an

Matches which subject

yo · tú · él/ella/usted · nosotros · ellos/ellas/ustedes
  1. Yo ______ (estudiar) para el examen.
  2. Nosotros ______ (escuchar) a la maestra.
  3. Mi hermano ______ (trabajar) en un café.
  4. Tú ______ (hablar) español muy bien.
  5. Ellos ______ (practicar) el béisbol.
  6. La maestra ______ (enseñar) ciencias.
1. estudio — yo → -o. (Yo estudio para el examen.)
2. escuchamos — nosotros → -amos. (Nosotros escuchamos a la maestra.)
3. trabaja — él → -a. (Mi hermano trabaja en un café.)
4. hablas — tú → -as. (Tú hablas español muy bien.)
5. practican — ellos → -an. (Ellos practican el béisbol.)
6. enseña — ella → -a. (La maestra enseña ciencias.)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two more sentences of your own about your real school day — one starting with Yo and one starting with Mis amigos y yo (nosotros). Use a different -ar verb in each, and read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same estudiar sentences you met on Day 2 — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your spelling, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Yo estudio español. (I study Spanish.) — yo → -o.
2
Write what you hear…
¿Tú estudias mucho? (Do you study a lot?) — note the opening ¿ and tú → -as.
3
Write what you hear…
Ella estudia en casa. (She studies at home.) — ella → -a.
4
Write what you hear…
Nosotros estudiamos juntos. (We study together.) — nosotros → -amos.
5
Write what you hear…
Ellos estudian ciencias. (They study science.) — ellos → -an.

Notice how the ending changed each time even though the verb stayed estudiar. Your ear is learning to catch that final sound — -o, -as, -a, -amos, -an.

🧠 PART 4 · ¿ACCIÓN O SER? 8 min

Two to finish on

Same question every time: is this what someone does (an -ar verb) or who someone is (ser)?

✅ TRY IT · choose the right verb
"Yo ______ inglés y español." (I ______ English and Spanish.)
✅ TRY IT · choose the right verb
"Mi maestra ______ muy amable." (My teacher ______ very kind.)

One to build yourself: how would you say "They work and study"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Ellos trabajan y estudian. Two actions → two -ar verbs; "they" → the -an ending on both. No ser needed.

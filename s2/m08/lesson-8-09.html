🎵 CULTURA · EL MARIACHI
La música que representa a México

El mariachi nació en el estado de Jalisco y hoy es un símbolo de México en todo el mundo. Un grupo de mariachi lleva guitarras, violines, trompetas y el guitarrón (un bajo grande). En 2011 la UNESCO lo declaró Patrimonio de la Humanidad. En una fiesta mexicana, cuando el mariachi está tocando, todos cantan.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

Un viaje por el mundo hispano — the Semester-2 VR project

For your Semester-2 VR project you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE for a guided viaje (journey) through several stops in the Spanish-speaking world — a Colombian plaza during a festival, a Buenos Aires café, an Andean market. At each stop you'll narrate: say what's happening around you, compare two places, and recount where you went before. You're not performing today — this is your preview.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO ON THE VIAJE
  1. Describir lo que pasa — say what's happening now. Present progressive: La gente está bailando; un músico está tocando la guitarra.
  2. Comparar los lugares — compare your stops. Esta plaza es más animada que la otra; la comida aquí es mejor.
  3. Contar tu viaje — recount where you went. Preterite (M6–7): Ayer fui a un festival y visité un mercado.
  4. Reaccionar — react with feeling. ¡Qué alegre! Me gusta más la cumbia que el tango.

Rehearse one line before you ever step in

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — think it through
You arrive at a festival stop and want to narrate the scene. Which line fits best?

Now build one line: at the Andean market, how would you say “A musician is playing and the market is bigger than the plaza”? Say it aloud, then reveal.

Un músico está tocando y el mercado es más grande que la plaza. Present progressive (está tocando) for the live action + a comparison (más grande que) — the two VR skills in one breath.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick “travel script”: pick three stops on today's music map and write one line for each — a progressive (what's happening), a comparison (this vs. that), and a preterite (what you did there). Keep it; you'll walk into the VR project with your lines ready.
🌐 CULTURA A FONDO · UN CONTINENTE, MUCHOS RITMOS

Think of the map you just explored as a single song with many verses. The same Spanish language stretches from the accordion-driven vallenato of Colombia to the panpipes of the Andes, from Cuban son to Mexican mariachi — each shaped by Indigenous, African, and European roots meeting in a different place. A viaje por el mundo hispano is really a journey through that mix. When your VR project takes you from stop to stop, you're not just practicing verbs; you're tracing how one language carries a whole hemisphere of stories.

★ ¿Qué está pasando? — What's Happening Right Now
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A few new verbs and comparison words — then your first new grammar: saying what's happening this very second.
First you'll add Set B: two action verbs (celebrar, tocar) and the little words that compare (más, menos, mejor, peor). Then you meet the present progressive — Spanish's way of saying “I am -ing” right now: estar + a verb ending in -ando or -iendo. Estoy bailando = “I am dancing.” You already own estar from earlier in the year — today you learn what to hang on the end of it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use celebrar and tocar, and the comparison words más, menos, mejor, peor.
2
I will be able to form the present progressive (estar + -ando / -iendo) to say what's happening now.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

Right this second…

In English you say “I am reading this lesson” — not just “I read.” That “am + -ing” means it's happening now. Spanish does the same thing, and by the end of this page you'll build it yourself. Let's warm up.

📚 PART 2 · SET B · VERBOS Y COMPARACIONES 11 min

Two verbs, four comparison words

Click 🔊 and say each one back. The two verbs are given in the infinitive (the “to…” form); the last four are comparison words you'll use tomorrow.

  • celebrarto celebrate
  • tocarto play (an instrument) / to touch
  • másmore
  • menosless
  • mejorbetter
  • peorworse

Note: tocar means “to play” only for instruments and music (tocar la guitarra). To play a sport or game you'll use jugar, which you met earlier this semester.

🧠 PART 3 · EL PRESENTE PROGRESIVO 16 min

The recipe: estar + -ando / -iendo

The present progressive has two parts. Part one is estar, which you already conjugate. Part two is the present participle — take the verb, drop the ending, and add -ando (for -ar verbs) or -iendo (for -er / -ir verbs).

VerbDrop & addParticipleWith estar
bailar (to dance)→ bail- + andobailandoEstoy bailando.
cantar (to sing)→ cant- + andocantandoEstán cantando.
comer (to eat)→ com- + iendocomiendoEstamos comiendo.
escribir (to write)→ escrib- + iendoescribiendoEstá escribiendo.

Listen to it living in a sentence

  • Estoy bailando en la fiesta.I am dancing at the party. (-ar → -ando)
  • Ellos están cantando una canción.They are singing a song. (-ar → -ando)
  • Estamos comiendo y celebrando.We are eating and celebrating. (-er → -iendo)

🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear sentences and decide what they mean. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. Ask the key question: is this happening now (-ando/-iendo) or a comparison (más/menos…que, tan…como)?

  • Estoy bailando en la fiesta.happening now → progressive
  • Ellos están cantando una canción.happening now → progressive
  • Estamos comiendo y celebrando.happening now → progressive
  • La cumbia es más alegre que el tango.comparison → más…que
  • Esta fiesta es menos grande que la otra.comparison → menos…que
  • Canto tan bien como mi hermana.comparison → tan…como

Hear the difference? A verb ending in -ando/-iendo = happening now. A frame with que or como = comparing. That single question is most of the quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · DOS ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first, then answer from what you heard.

  • Tu guitarra es mejor que la mía.the irregular comparative mejor
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In the clip, what is being said about the two guitars?
✅ DECIDE · now or comparing?
«Nosotros ___ comiendo y celebrando.» (We ___ eating and celebrating.)
✅ DECIDE · fill the comparative
«Esta fiesta es ___ grande ___ la otra.» (This party is less big than the other.)

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

HAPPENING NOW = estar + -ando/-iendo (-ar → -ando, -er/-ir → -iendo). COMPARING = más/menos + adj + que, the irregulars mejor/peor, or tan + adj + como (as…as).

🎵 CULTURA A FONDO · LA MÚSICA COMO MEMORIA

You end this module able to describe a fiesta as it happens and to weigh one rhythm against another — and that's exactly how music lives in the Spanish-speaking world: passed hand to hand, compared, argued over, and celebrated. From a Colombian cumbia to a Mexican mariachi to an Andean huayno, every style you met carries the memory of the people who made it. The verbs you practiced — está tocando, es más alegre que — are how you step into that living conversation. ¡Sigue escuchando!

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You've learned the two patterns. Today you use them — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the present progressive and comparatives, spaced a day after the first on purpose — that's how the patterns become automatic. There's no new grammar today. You'll make the choices yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation drawn from the exact clips you met on Days 2 and 3. It's all ungraded: mistakes here are how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to complete sentences with the present progressive and comparative frames, and explain why.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken sentence and write down what I hear, accents and all.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The two patterns, in one breath

Say them out loud before you write: happening now = estar + -ando / -iendo (Estoy bailando). Comparing = más/menos + adj + que, or mejor/peor, or tan + adj + como. Hold both in your head — you're about to make the calls yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which pattern feels more solid so far?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You make the choice

On paper or in a document, complete each sentence. Items 1–3 need a present participle (-ando / -iendo); items 4–6 need a comparative frame. Say why as you write, and commit to all six before you reveal the key.

HAPPENING NOW

estar + participle
-ar → -ando · -er/-ir → -iendo

COMPARING

más/menos + adj + que
mejor/peor · tan + adj + como
  1. Ahora yo estoy ______ (bailar) en la fiesta.
  2. Los músicos están ______ (tocar) el tambor.
  3. Nosotros estamos ______ (comer) y celebrando.
  4. La cumbia es ______ alegre ______ el tango. (more…than)
  5. Tu guitarra es ______ que la mía. (better)
  6. Yo canto ______ bien ______ mi hermana. (as…as)
1. bailando — bailar is -ar → -ando.
2. tocando — tocar is -ar → -ando.
3. comiendo — comer is -er → -iendo.
4. más … que — (La cumbia es más alegre que el tango.)
5. mejor — the irregular for “better” (never más bueno).
6. tan … como — (Yo canto tan bien como mi hermana.)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two sentences of your own about a real celebration: one that says what someone is doing right now (estar + -ando/-iendo) and one that compares two things in it. Read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same sentences you met on Days 2 and 3 — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as often as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your accents, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Estoy bailando en la fiesta. (I am dancing at the party.) — present progressive, -ar → -ando.
2
Write what you hear…
Ellos están cantando una canción. (They are singing a song.) — note the accents on están and canción.
3
Write what you hear…
Estamos comiendo y celebrando. (We are eating and celebrating.) — comer is -er → -iendo.
4
Write what you hear…
La cumbia es más alegre que el tango. (Cumbia is livelier than the tango.) — más…que.
5
Write what you hear…
Canto tan bien como mi hermana. (I sing as well as my sister.) — the equal frame tan…como.

Notice the mix: the first three are “happening now” (estar + participle); the last two compare. Your ear is learning to hear both patterns.

🧠 PART 4 · DOS PARA TERMINAR 8 min

Two to finish on

Ask the question each time: is this happening now (progressive) or a comparison?

✅ TRY IT · happening now
“The singer is playing the guitar.” — El cantante ___ ___ la guitarra.
✅ TRY IT · comparison
“This festival is worse than last year's.” Which word fits “worse”?

One to build yourself: how would you say “We are celebrating”? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Estamos celebrando. “We” → estamos; celebrar is -ar → celebrando.

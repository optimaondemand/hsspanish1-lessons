📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Today is the payoff — and the gate. You've met the regular preterite four times over two weeks; now you show it.
First you'll tune your ear with a short listening warm-up that reuses the very sentences you'll hear on the quiz — because part of the Grammar Quiz is listening. Then you'll take the quiz itself (30 points, 2 attempts). This is the Semester-2 gate: it protects the past-tense work Módulos 7–9 build on. Finally you'll do an honest “I can…” self-check and get a first look at Módulo 7. ¡A demostrarlo!

I will be able to hear a spoken preterite sentence and understand who did what, and when.

🔒 ABOUT THE GATE · READ THIS FIRST
This quiz is the Semester-2 gate, and here's exactly what that means — no surprises:
  • Score 80% or higher and you advance straight to Módulo 7 — no review plan needed. Your past-tense foundation is solid, and M7–M9 build directly on it.
  • Score below 80% and you'll get a short, targeted review plan before M7 — not a penalty, and not a mark against you. It's a couple of focused activities so the tense is steady before the harder past-tense work begins.
  • You have 2 attempts and your higher score counts, so a shaky first try is not the end of anything.
The gate exists to protect your learning, not to block you. The preterite is the ground the rest of the semester stands on — this just makes sure the ground is firm.
🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear preterite sentences and answer who did what. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask: which subject, and what did they do?

  • Ayer hablé con mi familia.yo, -ar
  • ¿Hablaste con el profesor?tú, -ar
  • Ella comió en un restaurante.ella, -er
  • Nosotros visitamos un museo ayer.nosotros, -ar
  • Ellos comieron pescado anoche.ellos, -er
  • La semana pasada viajé a España y visité Madrid.yo, two -ar verbs + time word

Hear the pattern? A time word or clear subject, then a stem + a past ending. That single question — who, and what did they do? — is the whole quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · DOS ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first, then answer below.

  • Ella comió en un restaurante.who + what did they do?
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In the clip, who ate at a restaurant?
✅ DECIDE · present or preterite?
«Anoche nosotros ___ en la fiesta.» (Last night we ___ at the party.) — bailar, past.
✅ DECIDE · which ending?
«Ayer ella (escribir) ___ una carta.»

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

Stem + ending. -ar → -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -aron. -er/-ir → -í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -ieron. If there's a past time word (ayer, anoche), it's the preterite — even when the nosotros form looks like the present.

📝 PART 3 · LA PRUEBA DE GRAMÁTICA 18 min

Grammar Quiz — the regular preterite

This is the graded quiz for Módulo 6 — the Semester-2 gate. Before you launch it, read what to expect:

  • 30 points total · 2 attempts (your higher score counts).
  • Includes 10–15 points of listening — short clips like the ones you just heard. Have your sound on.
  • The rest asks you to conjugate regular -ar and -er/-ir verbs in the preterite and tell past from present using time clues.
  • No new vocabulary — everything comes from this module.
  • 80% or higher advances you to Módulo 7 with no review plan; below 80% earns a short targeted review first (not a penalty).

🌎 ESPAÑA A FONDO
El español que cruzó el océano

The Spanish you just used the preterite in began in España and then spread — today more people speak Spanish in the Americas than in Spain itself, which is why our course keeps a Latin-American register. But the grammar you gated through today is shared by nearly 500 million speakers across two dozen countries. Viajé, comí, visitamos — the same endings work from Madrid to Mexico City to Buenos Aires. You just unlocked the past tense for an entire hemisphere.

★ ¿Qué hiciste? — Talking About the Past Begins
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Big question for this whole module: How do I talk about what I did?
Until now, almost everything you've said in Spanish has lived in the present. Starting this module, you get the past — the ability to say what you did, where you traveled, what you ate. That's a big step, so Módulo 6 is the gate the rest of the semester leans on: we build the past tense here, carefully and spaced out. Today there's no verb tense yet, on purpose. Instead you'll stock up on the words that signal the past — ayer, anoche, la semana pasada — plus a few travel nouns, so that tomorrow, when the preterite arrives, it has a place to land.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use past-time expressions like ayer, anoche, and la semana pasada to point to when something happened.
2
I will be able to name key travel words — el viaje, el boleto, la maleta — the setting for this module's stories.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Think back for a second

Before any Spanish, just remember something real: what did you actually do yesterday? Nothing fancy — woke up, ate, watched something, talked to someone. By the end of this module you'll be able to say those things in Spanish. Today we begin with the words that anchor a story in time.

📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the 18 words of Módulo 6 — the same words you'll meet on the listening-based Vocabulary Check (Day 7). Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 button and say each word back out loud before you flip. If you can hear the word and picture its meaning without reading, you're ready.

Tip: the verbs are all listed as infinitives (viajar = to travel). This module puts them into the preterite — viajé, viajaste, viajó… Learn the meaning here, then practice the past-tense forms in the lessons.

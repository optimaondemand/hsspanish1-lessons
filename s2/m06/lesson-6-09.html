🌎 CULTURA · ESPAÑA
Un puente entre continentes

España sits where Europe nearly touches Africa — only 14 km of sea separate them at the Strait of Gibraltar. That location shaped its history: Roman, Muslim, and Christian cultures all left their mark, and later Spanish ships carried the language across the Atlantic to the Americas. When you study Spanish, you're studying a language that bridges continents — which is exactly the journey the VR project below will take you on.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

Un viaje por el mundo hispano — your semester VR project

Later this semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, and arrive in a Spanish-speaking city during a festival (un festival). You'll move through it in Spanish: ask for directions, find a place to stay, order food — and at the end, recount what you did using the preterite. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so that when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE PROJECT
  1. Llegar — arrive at the city during its festival. Greet people and take in the fuegos artificiales, music, and crowds.
  2. Pedir direcciones — ask for directions. ¿Dónde está…? / Doble a la derecha / a la izquierda.
  3. Buscar alojamiento — find lodging. Necesito una habitación en el hotel.
  4. Pedir comida — order food. Quiero… / ¿Cuánto cuesta?
  5. Contar lo que hiciste — recount what you did. Use the preterite: «Llegué al festival, caminé por la plaza, y probé la comida.» This is the heart of the project.

Rehearse the ending — recounting your day

Imagine you just finished your festival day. How would you say “Yesterday I arrived at the festival and walked around the plaza” — using the preterite? Say it aloud, then reveal.

Ayer llegué al festival y caminé por la plaza. Both yo forms; caminé is a clean regular -ar preterite. (Llegué adds a small spelling tweak — you'll meet those in Módulo 7.) This kind of recounting is exactly what the project's final step asks for.

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick “festival script” for yourself: one line to arrive and greet, one to ask directions, one to find lodging, one to order food, and two past-tense lines recounting what you did (Caminé…, Probé…). Keep it — you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready.
🌎 ESPAÑA A FONDO · LOS FESTIVALES
Fiestas que llenan las calles

Spain is famous for its festivals: Las Fallas in Valencia, where giant sculptures are built and then burned; La Tomatina in Buñol, a massive tomato fight; the Feria de Abril in Sevilla, full of flamenco and horses. Across the Spanish-speaking world, festivals are where a city shows its character — and where, afterward, everyone has a story to tell in the past tense. That's why the VR project drops you into a festival: it's the perfect place to do things worth recounting.

Lesson 9.03
★ Normalmente… pero ayer… — Braiding Present & Past
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
This is the heart of the capstone: putting the two tenses in the same sentence.
Real speech mixes tenses constantly — «Normalmente vivo aquí, pero el verano pasado viajé a…» That contrast — what you usually do (present) versus what you did once (preterite) — is how you narrate an experience. Today you'll hear three model sentences that do exactly this, take them apart, and start building your own.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to hear a sentence and tell which verb is present and which is preterite — and why.
2
I will be able to build a «normalmente…, pero…» sentence that mixes present and preterite.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Two clocks, one sentence

Think of it as two clocks. One tells your routine (present); the other tells a specific past moment (preterite). Words like normalmente, todos los días, siempre point to the first; ayer, anoche, el verano pasado point to the second.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which "two clocks" story would you most like to tell about your own life?
🔊 PART 2 · TRES MODELOS 14 min

Listen, read, and spot the two tenses

Click each 🔊, follow along, and notice where the sentence switches from present (routine/description) to preterite (a completed past action).

  • «Normalmente vivo en Miami, pero el verano pasado viajé a México.»
  • «Mi ciudad es grande y bonita; ayer caminé por el centro y visité un museo.»
  • «Todos los días me levanto temprano, pero anoche me acosté muy tarde.»

Presente — routine / description

Triggers: normalmente, todos los días, siempre.

vivo · es · me levanto

Pretérito — a completed past action

Triggers: ayer, anoche, el verano pasado.

viajé · caminé · visité · me acosté
🧠 PART 3 · ANALIZA 10 min

Present or preterite?

Replay the clips above if you need to. For each, decide which tense the verb should be.

✅ CHOOSE THE TENSE
"Todos los días ___ a la escuela." (Every day I go…) — routine.
✅ CHOOSE THE TENSE
"El verano pasado mi familia y yo ___ a Costa Rica." (Last summer we went…) — one past trip.
✅ CHOOSE THE TENSE
"Mi pueblo ___ pequeño y tranquilo." (My town is small and quiet.) — a description.
✍️ PART 4 · CONSTRÚYELO 8 min

Your first «normalmente…, pero…»

Say a true sentence about yourself with this exact shape: Normalmente + present verb, pero + a past-time marker + preterite verb. Commit out loud before you reveal the model.

Say your sentence, then reveal a model to compare.

Normalmente estudio en casa, pero ayer estudié en la biblioteca. Present (estudio, routine) → preterite (estudié, a specific yesterday). Yours will be different — and true.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write a short three-sentence paragraph: sentence 1 describes your normal life (present), sentence 2 tells one thing you did recently (preterite), sentence 3 says what you're going to do next (ir a + infinitive). Keep it — it's the skeleton of your Day-8 recording.

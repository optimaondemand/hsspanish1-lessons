★ El presente, otra vez — Your Present-Tense Toolkit
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Módulo 9 — the last module of Spanish 1. The essential question for these two weeks: «Can I narrate and describe my experiences in Spanish?»
This whole module is a capstone: there is no new grammar and no new vocabulary. Instead you pull everything from the year into one place. Today we reassemble your present-tense toolkit — regular verbs, stem-changers, reflexive verbs, and ir a + infinitive for the near future. Tomorrow we do the same for the past. By Day 3 you'll braid them together to tell a real story about your own life.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to talk about what I do normally using regular, stem-changing, and reflexive verbs in the present.
2
I will be able to say what I am going to do with ir a + infinitive.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Look how far you've come

A year ago you could say almost nothing in Spanish. Now you can describe your routine, your town, your plans. This module is a victory lap — you already own the tools; we're just polishing them. Start by taking stock.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which present-tense tool do you reach for most naturally right now?
🧠 PART 2 · VERBOS REGULARES Y CON CAMBIO 12 min

Regular verbs & stem-changers — listen and read

Regular verbs keep their stem and swap endings by person. Stem-changers (from Módulo 2) shift a vowel in the middle when it's stressed — querer → quiero, poder → puedo, jugar → juego, dormir → duermo — but the nosotros form keeps the original vowel.

TypeExample (yo)Listen
Regular -arhablo, camino, viajo
Stem e→iequiero (querer)
Stem o→ueduermo (dormir)
Stem u→uejuego (jugar)
✅ TRY IT · pick the present form
"Yo ___ ir a la playa." (I want to go to the beach.) — querer, stem e→ie.
✅ TRY IT · pick the present form
"Nosotros ___ fútbol los domingos." (We play soccer…) — careful: nosotros keeps the original vowel.
🕗 PART 3 · LOS VERBOS REFLEXIVOS 10 min

Your daily routine — the reflexive pronoun

Reflexive verbs (Módulo 4) describe things you do to yourself — the pronoun goes before the verb: me, te, se, nos, se. Listen and repeat.

  • me levantoI get up
  • te levantasyou get up
  • se vistehe / she gets dressed
  • mi rutina…a full routine sentence

Your turn: how would you say "I get up early"? Say it out loud, then reveal.

(Yo) me levanto temprano. The reflexive pronoun me comes before the verb; temprano = early.
➡️ PART 4 · IR A + INFINITIVO 8 min

Talking about what you're going to do

To talk about the near future (Módulo 1), use a form of ir + a + an infinitive: Voy a viajar (I'm going to travel), Vamos a celebrar (We're going to celebrate).

  • voy a…I'm going to…
  • vamos a…we're going to…
✅ TRY IT · future with ir a
"Este verano yo ___ a viajar a México." (This summer I'm going to travel…)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Say three true present-tense sentences about a normal day: one regular verb (Camino a la escuela), one reflexive (Me levanto a las siete), and one ir a plan (Mañana voy a estudiar). Speaking them now is the warm-up for your capstone recording on Day 8.

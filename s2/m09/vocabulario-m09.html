📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
This capstone has no new vocabulary — instead, these are the high-frequency travel & experience words you'll want fresh for the cumulative Vocabulary Check (Day 7), the VR project (Day 9), and the Q4 Final Exam. Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip and check the English. But your real goal is to know them by ear: tap the 🔊 and say each word back out loud before you flip.

Tip: nouns are shown with their article (el, la) — learn each one glued to its article. Section C reviews the four grammar tools you'll narrate with.

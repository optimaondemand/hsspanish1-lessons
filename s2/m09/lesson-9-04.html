📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you braided the tenses. Today you use them — in a model story, in writing, and by ear.
First you'll meet Sofía, who describes her town, narrates a real trip she took, and says what she's going to do next — the exact shape of your capstone. Then you'll write your own short narrative and train your ear with a dictation built from the three model sentences you heard yesterday. No new grammar; it's all recombination.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to write a short narrative that describes a place and tells what I did there.
2
I will be able to listen to mixed-tense sentences and write down exactly what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The three moves of a good story

A simple narrative has three moves: describe (present — set the scene), narrate (preterite — what happened), and look ahead (ir a — what's next). Sofía's story below hits all three. Watch for them.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Of the three moves, which feels strongest for you today?
📖 PART 2 · LA EXPERIENCIA DE SOFÍA 12 min

Listen and follow along

Play Sofía's story · a description, a past trip, and a plan

«Hola, me llamo Sofía. Normalmente vivo en una ciudad grande y estudio mucho. Mi ciudad es bonita y siempre hay gente en las plazas. El verano pasado viajé con mi familia a Guatemala. Fue una experiencia increíble: visité unas ruinas antiguas, caminé por un mercado enorme y comí comida deliciosa. Una noche bailamos en un festival. Ahora estoy en casa otra vez, pero el próximo verano voy a volver a viajar — quiero conocer más del mundo hispano.»

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Point to each verb and name its job: vivo / estudio / es (present — her normal life), viajé / visité / caminé / comí / bailamos (preterite — the trip), voy a volver (ir a — her plan). Three moves, one paragraph.
✍️ PART 3 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 13 min

Write your own three-move narrative

On paper or in a document, write four to five sentences about a real (or imagined) trip or a memorable day. Follow Sofía's shape. Commit to your whole draft before you reveal the sentence-frames.

  1. Describe where you normally are (present + ser/estar).
  2. Name the trip / day and when it was (preterite + a past-time marker).
  3. Tell two things you did (two preterite verbs).
  4. End with what you're going to do next (ir a + infinitive).
1. Normalmente (yo) vivo / estoy en ______ .
2. El verano pasado / El año pasado (yo) fui / viajé a ______ .
3. Allí (yo) visité ______ y comí / caminé / baile… ______ .
4. Ahora estoy en casa, pero pronto voy a ______ .
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Read your narrative aloud twice, then trim it to about 30–45 seconds of speaking. This draft becomes your Tarea oral script on Day 8 — keep it somewhere safe.
🎧 PART 4 · DICTADO 10 min

Listen and write — three mixed-tense sentences

These are the three model sentences from yesterday. Click 🔊 (replay as needed), write exactly what you hear — mind the accent marks on the preterite verbs — then reveal to check.

1
Write what you hear…
Normalmente vivo en Miami, pero el verano pasado viajé a México. Present vivo → preterite viajé (accent on the é).
2
Write what you hear…
Mi ciudad es grande y bonita; ayer caminé por el centro y visité un museo. Description es → two preterites caminé, visité.
3
Write what you hear…
Todos los días me levanto temprano, pero anoche me acosté muy tarde. Reflexive present me levanto → reflexive preterite me acosté.

Notice: every sentence pairs a present verb (routine/description) with a preterite verb (one past action). That's the pattern your ear is learning.

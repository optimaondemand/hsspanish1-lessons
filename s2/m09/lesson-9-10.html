🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 9 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening. Warm up: click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. Ask the one question that matters — is the verb about a routine/description (present) or a completed past action (preterite)?

  • Normalmente vivo…, pero… viajé.present → preterite
  • Mi ciudad es…; ayer caminé y visité…description → two preterites
  • Todos los días me levanto…, pero anoche me acosté…reflexive present → reflexive preterite
  • fuiirregular preterite (ir/ser)
  • está en el trabajo, pero es profesorestar (where) + ser (who)

Hear the pattern? A time-marker tells you the clock: normalmente/todos los días → present; ayer/anoche/el verano pasado → preterite. That single question runs through the whole quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · TRES ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

✅ DECIDE · present or preterite?
«Ayer nosotros ___ a la playa.» (Yesterday we went…)
✅ DECIDE · ser or estar?
«Mi mejor amiga ___ de Guatemala.» (My best friend is from Guatemala.)
✅ DECIDE · which form?
«El verano pasado yo ___ un museo en Lima.» (Last summer I visited…)

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal.

Present = what happens normally / a description. Preterite = one completed past action (watch the accent: viajé, caminé, visitó). ser = who/what/origin; estar = where/how right now. The time-marker usually tells you which.
📝 PART 3 · LA PRUEBA DE GRAMÁTICA 16 min

Grammar Quiz — cumulative

This is the graded quiz for Módulo 9. It's cumulative — it can draw on the whole year. Before you launch it, read what to expect:

  • 30 points total · 2 attempts (your higher score counts).
  • Includes 10–15 points of listening — short clips like the ones you just heard. Sound on.
  • The rest asks you to choose the right tense (present vs. preterite), the right verb (ser vs. estar), and correct forms — across the year.
  • No new material — everything comes from Spanish 1.

🌟 CULTURA A FONDO · SEGUIR ADELANTE
¿Y ahora qué?

Finishing Spanish 1 is a real milestone — you now hold a working key to 21 countries and nearly 500 million speakers. The best way to keep it sharp is to use it: change your phone's language for a day, follow a Spanish-language music playlist, watch a show with Spanish subtitles, or say «buenos días» to a Spanish speaker near you. A language grows only when you speak it. El mundo hispano te espera.

Lesson 9.02
★ El pretérito, otra vez — Your Past-Tense Toolkit
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday: what you do. Today: what you did — the preterite, regular and irregular.
The preterite is the tense of completed past actions — ayer, anoche, el verano pasado. You built it in Módulos 6 (regular) and 7 (irregular). No new grammar today; we're pulling both back out so that tomorrow you can weave past and present into one story. Watch the accent marks — in the preterite they carry the whole meaning: hablo (I speak) vs. habló (he spoke).
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to form the regular preterite of -ar, -er, and -ir verbs.
2
I will be able to use the high-frequency irregular preterites ser/ir (fui), tener (tuve), hacer (hice), and estar (estuve).
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

What did you do yesterday?

The preterite lives with time-markers that point backward — ayer (yesterday), anoche (last night), el año pasado (last year). Hearing one of those is a signal that the verb should be in the past.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which past-time expression do you find easiest to remember?
🧠 PART 2 · EL PRETÉRITO REGULAR 12 min

Regular endings — two patterns

-ar verbs take one set of endings; -er and -ir verbs share another. Notice the accented yo and él/ella forms.

Subjecthablar (-ar)comer (-er/-ir)Listen
yohablécomí
túhablastecomiste
usted / él / ellahablócomió
nosotros/asvisitamoscomimos
ustedes / ellos / ellashablaroncomieron

Hear a full regular-preterite sentence.

✅ TRY IT · regular preterite
"Ayer yo ___ por el centro." (Yesterday I walked…) — caminar, yo.
✅ TRY IT · regular preterite
"Mis amigos ___ un museo." (My friends visited a museum.) — visitar, ellos.
🔥 PART 3 · EL PRETÉRITO IRREGULAR 12 min

The four you'll use most

Irregular preterites (Módulo 7) don't take accents on yo/él, and they change their stem. These four are the workhorses of any story:

  • fuiI went / I was (ir & ser share this)
  • tuveI had (tener)
  • hiceI did / I made (hacer)
  • estuveI was (estar)

Hear an irregular-preterite sentence. Also useful: fue (he/she went or was) and fuiste (you went) .

✅ TRY IT · irregular preterite
"El verano pasado yo ___ a Guatemala." (Last summer I went…) — ir, yo.

Your turn: how would you say "I had a great time" (lit. "I had much fun")? Try, then reveal.

Me divertí mucho — or, using tener: Tuve mucha diversión. The workhorse tuve (I had) has no accent and a changed stem.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Tell yourself, out loud, three true things you did yesterday — one regular -ar verb, one -er/-ir verb, and one irregular (fui / tuve / hice / estuve). This is the exact skill your capstone recording will ask for.

★ Un viaje por el mundo hispano — The VR Capstone
VR project · about 40 minutes · 100-pt unit project
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Today you don't preview the VR — you live it. This is the capstone project of the whole course.
You'll put on a headset, enter ENGAGE, and take «Un viaje por el mundo hispano» — a journey through a Latin-American city. You'll arrive, ask and give directions, check into lodging, order food, and — the capstone move — recount what you did (in the preterite) to someone at a festival. First a quick world-map review and a prep step; then you perform; then you reflect.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to navigate a Spanish-speaking city scene — greet, ask directions, check in, and order — in Spanish.
2
I will be able to recount what I did on my «trip» using the preterite, to a character at a festival.

🧩 PART 2 · PREPARACIÓN (PARTE 1 DEL PROYECTO) 10 min

Line up your phrases before you enter

The VR scene moves fast, so walk in ready. Here are the four moments and the language each one needs. Rehearse them aloud now.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE SCENE
  1. Llegar — arrive & greet. «Buenos días. Acabo de llegar a la ciudad.» Choose usted with an official.
  2. Pedir/dar direcciones — ask & give directions. «¿Dónde está el hotel?» — «Siga derecho y doble a la derecha en la esquina.»
  3. Registrarse — check into lodging. «Tengo una reservación. Quisiera una habitación, por favor.»
  4. Pedir comida — order food. «Quisiera el pescado, por favor. ¿Cuánto cuesta?»
  5. Contar — recount at the festival (in the preterite). «Hoy caminé por el centro, visité un museo y comí en un restaurante. ¡Fue increíble!»
  • a la derechato the right
  • la esquinathe corner
  • el hotelthe hotel
  • el mercadothe market

At the festival, how would you say "I walked and I ate"? Say it, then reveal.

Caminé y comí. Two regular preterites — the exact move the festival character is waiting to hear.
🥾 PART 3 · LA PERFORMANCE EN VR (PARTE 2 DEL PROYECTO) 15 min

«Un viaje por el mundo hispano» — step in and go

Enter the ENGAGE scenario. Move through all five moments in order. Speak out loud — the point is to use Spanish in a lived situation, not to be perfect. Your teacher captures a spatial recording of the session so it can be scored and shared in a class gallery walk.

🎤 THE CAPSTONE MOMENT
At the festival stall, a character asks: «¿Qué hiciste hoy?» (What did you do today?). Answer with at least three preterite verbs about your journey — this is the heart of the project, where a year of Spanish comes together in one spoken paragraph.

🌎 CULTURA · EL MUNDO HISPANO
Una ciudad, muchas culturas

The VR city is a composite of the Spanish-speaking world: a colonial plaza like those in México or Perú, a bustling mercado like the Caribbean's, a festival with drums and dancing that could be a Colombian carnaval or a Mexican feria. Wherever you «travel,» two courtesies hold everywhere: greet before you ask, and use usted with officials and elders. Courtesy is the passport that works in all 21 countries.

📝 PART 4 · REFLEXIÓN (PARTE 3 DEL PROYECTO) 7 min

Recount your journey — in writing

After the session, write a short reflection (submitted with the project): 4–5 sentences in the preterite recounting what you did on your VR journey, plus one sentence in English on what felt easiest and hardest. Use the frames if you need them.

1. Hoy llegué a ______ (city).
2. Pregunté dónde estaba el hotel y caminé por ______ .
3. En el restaurante pedí / comí ______ .
4. En el festival hablé con una persona y bailé.
5. ¡Fue una experiencia increíble!
💭 REFLECT · no wrong answer
Which moment of the journey did you feel most confident speaking?

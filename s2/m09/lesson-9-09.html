📖 PART 3 · PARTES B Y C 14 min

Reading and grammar, the way the exam asks them

On the exam, Part B gives you a short passage and asks about it, and Part C asks you to write one missing verb form. Here is one of each.

📖 PARTE B · LA LECTURA

Me llamo Diego. Normalmente me levanto a las siete, pero el sábado pasado me levanté a las diez. Fui al mercado con mi madre y compré fruta. Después caminamos por la plaza y visitamos un museo pequeño. Comí pescado en un restaurante cerca del centro. Fue un día mejor que un día normal.

📖 PARTE B · ITEM 1
What did Diego do last Saturday?
📖 PARTE B · ITEM 2
Why does the passage use me levanto and then me levanté?
✎ PARTE C · LA GRAMÁTICA

Write the missing verb form. One item from each módulo the exam covers.

Ayer nosotros ______ por el centro. (caminar) M6
Ella ______ muchas fotos. (hacer, pretérito) M7

On the exam, a missing accent never costs you the point — but the accent is part of the word, so write it if you can.

✍️ PART 4 · PARTE D 8 min

Writing a given sentence

Part D gives you an English sentence and asks you to write it in Spanish. It always tells you which structure to use. Two here; there are four on the exam.

Write in Spanish: “Yesterday I visited a museum.”
Write in Spanish: “I am going to travel this summer.”

The checker accepts a different word order if the structure is right. On the exam a person marks this, and they are looking for the structure, not perfection.

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the rules. Today you use them — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with saber/conocer and the direct-object pronouns, a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how a choice becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. You'll make the calls yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation using the exact clips from Lesson 5.03. It's all ungraded: mistakes here are how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose saber or conocer, and the right pronoun, to complete a sentence.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken sentence with these structures and write down what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The rules, in one breath

Say the two shortcuts aloud before you write: saber = facts & skills (how to); conocer = people & places (familiar with). And a pronoun (lo/la/los/las) replaces a thing you've already named — matching its gender and number, sitting before the verb.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which of yesterday's two ideas feels shakier right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You make the choice

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank. Items 1–4 want the correct form of saber or conocer; items 5–6 want the correct pronoun (lo, la, los, las). Say why as you write. Commit to all six before you reveal — the thinking is the practice.

SABER — facts & skills

Information, and how to do something.
sé · sabes · sabe · sabemos · saben

CONOCER — people & places

Familiar with someone or somewhere.
conozco · conoces · conoce · conocemos · conocen
  1. Yo ______ cocinar tortilla española. (saber / conocer)
  2. Nosotros ______ un buen mercado en Sevilla. (saber / conocer)
  3. ¿______ (tú) a mi amigo Diego? (saber / conocer)
  4. Ella ______ dónde está el supermercado. (saber / conocer)
  5. ¿La fruta? Yo ______ compro en el mercado. (lo / la / los / las)
  6. ¿Los huevos? El camarero ______ pone en la mesa. (lo / la / los / las)
1. sé — a skill (how to cook) → saber.
2. conocemos — a place we're familiar with → conocer.
3. Conoces — a person (note the personal a) → conocer.
4. sabe — information (where it is) → saber.
5. la — replaces la fruta (fem. sing.): La compro.
6. los — replaces los huevos (masc. pl.): Los pone.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two more sentences about your real life: one that must use saber or conocer (a skill you have, or a place/person you know), and one that answers a food question with a pronoun (¿Comes verduras? — Sí, las como). Read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same clips you met in Lesson 5.03 — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your accents, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Yo sé cocinar. (I know how to cook.) — saber + infinitive = a skill.
2
Write what you hear…
Yo conozco un buen restaurante. (I know a good restaurant.) — a place → conocer.
3
Write what you hear…
¿Sabes dónde está el mercado? (Do you know where the market is?) — information → saber; note the accents on dónde and está.
4
Write what you hear…
¿La fruta? La compro en el mercado. (The fruit? I buy it at the market.) — la replaces la fruta.
5
Write what you hear…
¿Los huevos? Los pongo en la mesa. (The eggs? I put them on the table.) — los replaces los huevos.

Notice how the pronoun always matches the food it stands for — that agreement is exactly what your ear is learning to expect.

🧠 PART 4 · ¿CUÁL ES CORRECTO? 8 min

Two to finish on

Same questions every time: fact/skill (saber) or person/place (conocer)? And does the pronoun match the thing?

✅ TRY IT · choose the verb
"Yo no ______ a la nueva camarera todavía." (I don't ______ the new waitress yet.)
✅ TRY IT · which pronoun?
"¿El postre? Mi hermano ______ prepara." (The dessert? My brother makes ______.)

One to build yourself: say "Do you (tú) know how to make paella?" Say it out loud first, then reveal.

¿Sabes hacer paella? A skill (how to) → saber; tú → sabes; + infinitive hacer.

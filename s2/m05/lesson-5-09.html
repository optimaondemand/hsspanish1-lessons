Europa. Spain sits on the Iberian Peninsula in southwestern Europe, sharing it with Portugal — the only Spanish-speaking country in Europe, and the origin of the language.
🌎 CULTURA · ESPAÑA
Un país de regiones

España is made of distinct regions, each with its own food and character: Andalucía in the south (Sevilla, flamenco, gazpacho), Cataluña in the northeast (Barcelona), the País Vasco with its famous pintxos, and Galicia in the northwest, known for seafood. Some regions even have their own languages alongside Spanish — catalán, euskera, gallego. One country, many tables.

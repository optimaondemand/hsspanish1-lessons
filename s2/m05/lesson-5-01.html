★ En el supermercado — Food at the Market
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Módulo 5. Our big question all module long: ¿Cómo compro y hablo de la comida? — How do I shop for and talk about food?
Today we start with the easy half: food you buy. You already know some food from Semester 1 (el arroz, el pollo, la fruta, las verduras…). Now you'll add ten market words — meat, fish, cheese, eggs, and more — plus two words to judge it all: rico (tasty) and caro (expensive). There is no new grammar today on purpose: we're stocking the pantry with words so that tomorrow's grammar has something real to talk about.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name common foods you buy at the market, each with its article.
2
I will be able to describe food as rico (tasty) or caro (expensive).
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Picture your kitchen

Before any Spanish, think about the food in your home right now — what's in the fridge, what's on the counter, what you'd buy if you went shopping today. By the end of this module you'll be able to shop for all of it and say what you think of it. Let's start with what you already like to eat.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
If you went to the market right now, what would you most want to buy?
📚 PART 2 · LA COMIDA DEL MERCADO 14 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 and say each word back out loud. Notice the article (la, el, los) — learn each food with its article, just like in Semester 1. The last two words describe food rather than name it.

  • el supermercadosupermarket
  • la carnemeat
  • el pescadofish (to eat)
  • el quesocheese
  • los huevoseggs
  • la manzanaapple
  • el tomatetomato
  • el postredessert

Two words to judge the food

  • rico/atasty / delicious
  • caro/aexpensive

Note: rico and caro are adjectives, so they agree with the food — la carne es rica, el pescado es caro. And remember el pescado is fish on your plate; a live fish in the water is el pez.

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Open your fridge or pantry and name out loud everything you can in Spanish — today's words plus the Semester 1 foods (el arroz, el pollo, la leche, el pan, la fruta, las verduras). For each one, add a judgment: ¡Qué rico! or Es caro.
✅ PART 3 · A PRACTICAR 12 min

Match the meaning

✅ TRY IT · which food fits?
You want to make an omelette. The main ingredient you need is:
✅ TRY IT · which word fits?
The steak costs a lot of money. You'd say the meat is:
✅ TRY IT · which article?
Which is written correctly — with the right article?

Your turn: how would you say "the fish is delicious" in Spanish? Say it out loud, then reveal.

El pescado es rico. Pescado is masculine, so the adjective is rico (not rica). Keep the article glued to the noun as you learn it.
🌎 CULTURA · ESPAÑA
Bienvenido a España

This module our featured country is España, the country where the Spanish language was born, on the Iberian Peninsula in Europe. Its capital is Madrid, but all module long we'll spend time in the sunny southern city of Sevilla (Seville), famous for its lively food markets and its tapas. Spanish cooking leans on the very foods on today's list — el pescado, el queso, los huevos, el tomate — and one dish you'll meet this week uses several at once: the tortilla española.

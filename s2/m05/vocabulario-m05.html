📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the 18 words of Módulo 5 — the same words you'll meet on the listening-based Vocabulary Check (Day 7). Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 button and say each word back out loud before you flip. If you can hear the word and picture its meaning without reading, you're ready.

Tip: learn each food glued to its article (el, la, los). Adjectives like rico and caro agree with the food (la carne es rica). And remember the two “knows”: saber = facts/skills, conocer = people/places.

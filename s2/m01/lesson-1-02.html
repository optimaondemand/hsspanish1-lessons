Lesson 1.02
★ Los planes — and Meet the Verb ir
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A few "plans" words, and the one verb this whole module is built on: ir (to go).
Today you meet the verb ir and its five forms — voy, vas, va, vamos, van. We're learning the forms by themselves first, the way you did with estar last semester. Tomorrow they snap into the module's one new structure (ir a + a verb). For now: hear them, say them, and get comfortable with the shapes.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use the "plans" words: el plan, la playa, ir, luego, hoy.
2
I will be able to recognize and say the five present-tense forms of ir: voy, vas, va, vamos, van.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

A quick warm-up

Yesterday: the weather. Today: what you'll do about it. Think of one place you like to go when the weather is nice — the park, the beach, a friend's house. Hold it in mind; you'll be naming it in Spanish in a minute.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
When it's sunny and warm, where do you like to go?
📚 PART 2 · LOS PLANES 10 min

Five words for making plans

Click the 🔊 and say each one back. Hoy and luego are the little time words that make a plan feel real: today and later.

  • el planthe plan
  • la playathe beach
  • irto go
  • hoytoday
  • luegolater / then
🧠 PART 3 · EL VERBO IR · SUS FORMAS 14 min

Meet ir — five forms, learned by ear

ir means "to go," and it's a little irregular — but there are only five forms and they're short. Listen to each, then say it back. Notice they all start with v.

SubjectirExample · listen
yovoyYo voy al parque.
túvas¿Vas a la playa?
usted / él / ellavaElla va con su familia.
nosotros/asvamosVamos a practicar.
ustedes / ellos / ellasvanEllos van en verano.

(In Latin America, ustedes covers "you all" everywhere; van is its form. Vosotros — Spain's informal "you all" — you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
One small useful phrase: a + el squeezes into al. So "to the park" is al parque, not "a el parque." Say aloud: Voy al parque · Voy a la playa. You'll use this every day this module.

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
No new grammar today — just pulling it back out of your head, where real learning happens.
This is your fourth meeting with ir a, and the first day of Week 2. Today you retrieve: an ear-quiz on the weather and seasons, a short production round, and a big spiral that lines up your Semester-1 verbs — ser, estar, tener, gustar — next to this week's ir a + verb. Choosing the right tool from all of them is exactly what the Day-10 quiz will ask.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize spoken weather and season words and match them to meaning.
2
I will be able to choose among ser, estar, tener, gustar, and ir a depending on the job the sentence does.

🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · CINCO HERRAMIENTAS 10 min

Five tools, five jobs

Here's your whole toolkit so far. They are not interchangeable — each does a different job. Listen and read across the rows:

Tool (from)Its jobExample · listen
ser (S1)who / what someone is · originYo soy de aquí.
estar (S1)location · how they feel nowYo estoy en casa.
tener (S1)what someone has · ageYo tengo quince años.
gustar (S1)what someone likesMe gusta el verano.
ir a + verb (this module)what someone is going to doYo voy a nadar.

✍️ PART 4 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check your verb choice and spelling.

1. Say: “Today it's sunny, so I'm going to go to the park.”

Hoy hace sol, entonces voy a ir al parque. Weather (hace sol) then a plan (voy a ir). Note al = a + el.

2. Say: “It's cold and my family is going to eat at home.”

Hace frío y mi familia va a comer en casa. "Mi familia" (ella) → va a + comer.

3. Say: “I'm from the U.S., I like summer, and this weekend I'm going to swim.”

Soy de los Estados Unidos, me gusta el verano, y este fin de semana voy a nadar. Three tools in one breath: ser (origin), gustar (what I like), ir a (plan).

🇦🇷 CULTURA · MAR DEL PLATA
La playa de los argentinos

When Valentina's family says «vamos a la playa,» they likely mean Mar del Plata, Argentina's most famous beach city, about five hours south of Buenos Aires. In January — the peak of summer — millions of porteños head there for sun and sea. It's the southern-hemisphere version of a July beach trip in the U.S.

🇦🇷 CULTURA A FONDO · ARGENTINA

Argentina gave the world the tango, born in the neighborhoods of Buenos Aires over a century ago and now danced everywhere. It's more than a dance — it's a whole style of music and poetry, and UNESCO lists it as part of humanity's cultural heritage. When you plan a trip in Spanish, a milonga (a tango dance hall) is one of the Buenos Aires places you might visit.

📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the 18 words of Módulo 1 — the same words you'll meet on the listening-based Vocabulary Check (Day 7). Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 and say each word back out loud before you flip. If you can hear the word and picture its meaning without reading, you're ready.

Tip: most weather uses hace + a noun (hace sol, hace calor) — learn it as a whole chunk. Llueve and nieva are one-word verbs. Nouns are shown with their article (la, el) — learn each glued to its article.

🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear plans and decide who's going and what they'll do. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask: which form of ir is it, and what's the action after a?

  • Voy a estudiar mañana.yo → voy a + estudiar
  • ¿Vas a ir a la playa?tú → vas a + ir
  • Ella va a comer con su familia.ella → va a + comer
  • Vamos a practicar español.nosotros → vamos a + practicar
  • Ellos van a viajar en verano.ellos → van a + viajar
  • Hoy hace sol, pero mañana va a llover.weather now + weather forecast

Hear the shape? A form of ir, the little a, then an unchanged verb — every time. That's most of the quiz.

  • Yo voy al parque cuando hace buen tiempo.a plan tied to the weather

✅ DECIDE · which form of ir?
«Nosotros ___ viajar en verano.» (We're going to travel in summer.)
✅ DECIDE · correctly built?
Which is correct?

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

Near future = a form of ir (voy/vas/va/vamos/van) + a + an unchanged verb. The a is never optional, and the second verb never changes. Weather stays hace + noun (or llueve / nieva).

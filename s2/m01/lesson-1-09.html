🌎 CULTURA · EL CONO SUR
Cuatro países, un extremo del mundo

El Cono Sur — Argentina, Chile, Uruguay y Paraguay — is the southern tip of South America, so far south that Chile's Patagonia nearly touches Antarctica. Because it's below the equator, the whole region runs on flipped seasons: enero is summer, julio is winter. The Andes run down Argentina's western edge, and just across them lies Chile — a long, thin country pressed between mountains and the Pacific.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

Un viaje por el mundo hispano — your semester VR project

This semester's big VR project puts you inside a trip. You'll put on a headset, step into ENGAGE, and arrive in a Spanish-speaking city during a festival. Over the quarter you'll ask for directions, find lodging, order food, and — later — recount what you did. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so the moves feel familiar when the session comes.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO ACROSS THE PROJECT
  1. Llegar — arrive in the city during a festival. Greet people and take in the scene: ¡Hay una fiesta!
  2. Pedir direcciones — ask for directions. ¿Dónde está…? · ¿Cómo llego a…?
  3. Buscar alojamiento — find a place to stay. Necesito una habitación.
  4. Pedir comida — order food at a café or stall. Quiero… / Me gustaría…
  5. Contar — recount what you did afterward (later this semester).

This module gives you the first tool for that trip: planning. Before you travel, you say what you're going to do — and ir a + a verb is exactly how.

🇦🇷 CULTURA A FONDO · ARGENTINA

If the VR trip lands in Argentina during a festival, it might be Carnaval in the northern provinces, the grape-harvest Fiesta de la Vendimia in Mendoza, or a neighborhood peña with live folk music and dancing. Festivals across the Spanish-speaking world are where a city shows its heart — and arriving with a few planning phrases («Voy a ver el desfile», «Vamos a bailar») turns you from a spectator into a participant.

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the formula. Today you use it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with ir a + infinitive, landing a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how a structure becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. You'll make the future yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation. It's all ungraded: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to complete sentences with the right form of ir a + a verb and explain the choice.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken plan and write down exactly what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The formula, in one breath

Before you write, say the formula out loud: a form of ir (voy/vas/va/vamos/van) + a + an unchanged verb. The form of ir matches the subject; the second verb stays in its "to…" shape. Hold that in mind — you're about to build it yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
How comfortable is ir a + verb feeling after yesterday?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You build the future

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank with the correct form of ir a (voy a, vas a, va a, vamos a, van a). The verb after it is already in place — don't change it. Say why to yourself as you write. Commit to all six before you reveal the key.

voy a · vas a · va a · vamos a · van a  +  infinitive
  1. Yo ______ estudiar esta noche.
  2. Nosotros ______ viajar en verano.
  3. Mi hermana ______ ir a la playa mañana.
  4. ¿Tú ______ practicar español hoy?
  5. Ellos ______ comer en un restaurante luego.
  6. Cuando llueve, yo ______ leer en casa.
1. voy a — "yo" → voy. (Yo voy a estudiar esta noche.)
2. vamos a — "nosotros" → vamos. (Nosotros vamos a viajar en verano.)
3. va a — "mi hermana" (ella) → va. (Mi hermana va a ir a la playa.)
4. vas a — "tú" → vas. (¿Tú vas a practicar español hoy?)
5. van a — "ellos" → van. (Ellos van a comer en un restaurante luego.)
6. voy a — "yo" → voy. (Cuando llueve, yo voy a leer en casa.)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two more sentences of your own about your real weekend — each with a weather word and a plan: Si hace sol, voy a… / Si hace frío, voy a… Read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five plans

These are the same ir a sentences you met on Day 3 — now the training wheels are off. For each: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind the little a and the accent marks, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Voy a estudiar mañana. (I'm going to study tomorrow.) — voy a + the infinitive estudiar.
2
Write what you hear…
¿Vas a ir a la playa? (Are you going to go to the beach?) — note the opening ¿ and that ir itself is the infinitive here.
3
Write what you hear…
Ella va a comer con su familia. (She's going to eat with her family.) — va a + comer.
4
Write what you hear…
Vamos a practicar español. (We're going to practice Spanish.) — vamos a + practicar; mind the ñ in español.
5
Write what you hear…
Ellos van a viajar en verano. (They're going to travel in summer.) — van a + viajar.

Notice every sentence used the same skeleton: a form of ir + a + an unchanged verb. That's the pattern your ear is learning to expect.

🧠 PART 4 · DOS MÁS 8 min

Two to finish on

Same question every time: does the form of ir match the subject, and did the second verb stay an infinitive?

✅ TRY IT · complete the plan
"Mis amigos y yo ______ nadar cuando hace calor." (My friends and I are going to swim when it's hot.)
✅ TRY IT · spot the correct build
Which is correct?

One to build yourself: how would you say "I'm going to read at home"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Voy a leer en casa. "I" → voy a; then the infinitive leer; en casa = at home.

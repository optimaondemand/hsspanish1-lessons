Lesson 1.01 · Welcome to Semester 2
★ ¿Qué tiempo hace? — Weather & Seasons
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome back. This module's big question: «¿Qué tiempo hace, y qué voy a hacer»? — What's the weather like, and what am I going to do?
Semester 2 opens by bringing everything back. You already own ser, estar, tener, gustar and a room full of present-tense verbs — this module reactivates all of it and then adds one new tool. Today we start with the easy, useful half: the weather and the seasons. There's no new grammar today on purpose. We're stocking up on words — and reawakening the Semester-1 Spanish you'll build on all week.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to say what the weather is like using hace sol / hace calor / llueve / nieva and name the four seasons.
2
I will be able to reactivate my Semester-1 verbs (ser, estar, tener, gustar, and present-tense actions) in new sentences.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Look out your window

Before any Spanish, notice the weather right where you are. Is it bright, gray, hot, cold? In this module you'll be able to describe all of it — and say what you're going to do about it. Let's start by naming what's outside your window.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
What's the weather like where you are right now?
📚 PART 2 · EL CLIMA Y LAS ESTACIONES 14 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 and say each expression back out loud. Notice the pattern: most weather is hace + a noun (literally "it makes…"). Two are single verbs: llueve (it rains) and nieva (it snows). Learn them as whole set expressions — don't translate word by word.

  • el climathe weather / climate
  • hace solit's sunny
  • hace calorit's hot
  • hace fríoit's cold
  • hace vientoit's windy
  • llueveit rains / it's raining
  • nievait snows / it's snowing
  • nubladocloudy (está nublado)

Las estaciones — the four seasons

  • la estaciónthe season
  • la primaveraspring
  • el veranosummer
  • el otoñoautumn / fall
  • el inviernowinter
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Say the weather for each season out loud, even in fragments: En el verano hace calor. En el invierno hace frío y a veces nieva. En la primavera llueve. What's the weather like in your favorite season?
🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · TU ESPAÑOL DEL SEMESTRE 1 10 min

Wake up the verbs you already own

You didn't start from zero this semester. Here are your four Semester-1 workhorses — listen, read across, and notice each still does its own job. You'll lean on all four this module.

VerbIts jobExample · listen
serwho / what someone is · originYo soy de los Estados Unidos.
estarwhere someone is · how they feel nowYo estoy en casa.
tenerwhat someone has · ageYo tengo quince años.
-ar verban action someone doesYo estudio español.
✅ SPIRAL · which verb does the job?
"Yo ___ cansado hoy porque hace calor." (I ___ tired today because it's hot.) — a right-now feeling.
✅ SPIRAL · which verb does the job?
"Nosotros ___ de Argentina." (We ___ from Argentina.) — origin.

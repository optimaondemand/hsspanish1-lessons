📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Módulo 3. The big question all module long: ¿Cómo me muevo por la ciudad? — How do I get around a city?
Today we start with the map before the movement: the places in a city — the bank, the market, the museum, the plaza. There's no new grammar today on purpose. You're stocking up on the nouns so that tomorrow, when directions and the module's verbs arrive, you already know where you're going. Our featured country this module is México, so picture the streets of la Ciudad de México as you learn.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name nine common places in a city in Spanish, with their articles.
2
I will be able to recognize each place by ear and say where I would go to do something.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Picture your errands

Before any Spanish, think about a normal Saturday in a city: you take out money, you buy food, maybe you visit somewhere fun. Each of those happens in a lugar (a place). This module gives you the names for all of them — starting now.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
If you had a free afternoon downtown, where would you head first?
📚 PART 2 · LOS LUGARES DE LA CIUDAD 16 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 and say each word back out loud. Learn every place with its article (el, la) — that's the grammatical gender from Módulo 0 doing its job.

  • el centrodowntown / the center
  • la callethe street
  • la tiendathe shop / store
  • el bancothe bank
  • el mercadothe market
  • el restaurantethe restaurant
  • el museothe museum
  • la plazathe square / plaza
  • el hospitalthe hospital
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Think about your own town. Which of these nine places is cerca (close) and which is lejos (far)? Say a fragment out loud for each: el banco… cerca, el museo… lejos. (You met cerca and lejos before — they come back tomorrow.)
✅ PART 3 · A PRACTICAR 12 min

Where would you go?

Read each situation and pick the place that fits. The correct answer moves around — read all the options.

✅ TRY IT · which place fits?
You need to take out some cash. You go to…
✅ TRY IT · which place fits?
You want to buy fresh fruit and vegetables. You go to…
✅ TRY IT · which place fits?
You want to eat a nice meal out with friends. You go to…

Your turn: how would you say "the museum" in Spanish? Say it out loud, then reveal.

el museo. Masculine, so el — keep the article glued to the noun as you learn it.

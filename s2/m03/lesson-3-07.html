✍️ PART 3 · EVALUACIÓN 2: LENGUA EN ACCIÓN 17 min

Lengua en acción · 20 points · structured writing

Here you produce the language. You'll write a few short, guided sentences about what you do around a city — deliberately using the irregular-yo verbs and the direction words. The prompts tell you exactly what each sentence should do; you supply the Spanish.

The idea being graded is the one you've practiced all week: the special yo form for yo, regular forms for everyone else.

YO — special form

What I do.

Yo salgo de casa…
Yo veo el museo…

DIRECCIONES — getting around

How to get there.

Doblo a la derecha…
…derecho dos cuadras.

Before you open it, hear a model one more time. This is the kind of sentence your writing should echo:

  • Yo hago…«Yo hago mis tareas en el centro.»
  • Yo salgo…«Yo salgo de casa a las ocho.»

🇲🇽 MÉXICO A FONDO · PEDIR DIRECCIONES
Con cortesía se llega lejos

In México, if you're lost, it's completely normal to stop someone and ask: «Disculpe, ¿cómo llego a la plaza?» People are famously willing to help — often walking you part of the way. A polite disculpe (excuse me) and gracias go a long way. The very words you're being tested on today — a la derecha, derecho, dos cuadras — are the ones a stranger will use to point you home. That's the honest test of this module: can you understand the answer?

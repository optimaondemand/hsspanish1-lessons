🌎 CULTURA · UN PAÍS ENORME
México de norte a sur

México stretches from the deserts of the north to tropical beaches and jungle in the south. It's the most populous Spanish-speaking country in the world and home to 32 states. Beyond the capital, cities like Guadalajara (home of mariachi), Oaxaca (famous for food and crafts), and Mérida (in the Maya region) each have their own flavor — but everywhere, you can ask «¿Cómo llego a…?» and follow the answer.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA · UN VIAJE POR EL MUNDO HISPANO 14 min

Your semester VR project

Later this quarter you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, for Un viaje por el mundo hispano — a journey to a Spanish-speaking city in the middle of a festival. You'll arrive, find your way around, settle in, eat, celebrate — and afterward you'll recount what you did. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so you already know the moves when the session comes.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO ON THE TRIP
  1. Llegar a la ciudad — arrive and greet. Step off the bus and greet people politely («Buenos días», usted with elders).
  2. Pedir y seguir direcciones — ask and follow directions. This module, exactly: «¿Cómo llego a la plaza?» → «Derecho dos cuadras y a la derecha en el semáforo.»
  3. Buscar alojamiento — find a place to stay. Ask for a hotel or hostel near the center.
  4. Pedir comida — order food. At a market stall or café: «Quiero…», «¿Cuánto cuesta?»
  5. Disfrutar el festival — enjoy the festival. Music, food, and a plaza full of people.
  6. Contar tu viaje — recount it afterward. Later you'll tell the story of what you did — «Salgo del hotel, doy un paseo, veo el festival…»

Rehearse: asking the way

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — think it through
You're lost near the plaza and stop a friendly older shopkeeper. How do you begin?

(In Latin America, ustedes is “you all” for any group. Vosotros, Spain's informal “you all,” you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

Rehearse one line before you ever step in

A vendor asks where you're going. Say “I leave the hotel and I take a stroll through the plaza” — using two irregular-yo verbs. Say it aloud, then reveal.

Salgo del hotel y doy un paseo por la plaza. Salgo (salir) and doy (dar) — both special yo forms, exactly what tomorrow's quiz asks for.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick “trip script”: write one line for each step — a greeting (with usted), a direction question, a request for lodging, a food order, and one sentence recounting what you do (irregular yo). Keep it; you'll walk into the VR session with your lines ready.
🇲🇽 MÉXICO A FONDO · LAS FIESTAS
Una ciudad de fiesta

Your VR festival draws on México's rich calendar of celebrations. The Día de los Muertos (Nov. 1–2) fills plazas with marigolds, altars (ofrendas), and music honoring loved ones — recognized by UNESCO as a cultural treasure. Independence night (el Grito, Sept. 15) packs the Zócalo with tens of thousands. Courtesy matters at a fiesta: greet, say gracias, and join in. When you enter the VR festival, greet first — courtesy travels in any language.

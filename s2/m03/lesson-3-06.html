★ Repaso y espiral — Retrieval Before the Quiz
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
No new grammar today — just pulling it back out of your head, which is where real learning happens.
This is your fourth meeting with the irregular-yo verbs and the first day of Week 2. Today you retrieve: an ear-quiz on the city and direction words, a short production task, and a spiral that lines up three tools side by side — ir a + infinitive (from earlier this semester: voy a doblar a la derecha), the stem-changing verbs like querer and poder, and this module's irregular-yo verbs. Bringing them together is exactly what the Day-10 quiz will ask of you.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize spoken city and direction words and match them to meaning.
2
I will be able to combine ir a + infinitive, a stem-changer, and an irregular-yo verb in one description.

🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · TRES HERRAMIENTAS 10 min

Three tools, three jobs

You've built up several ways to talk about moving around. They do different jobs — listen and read across the row:

Tool (from)Its jobExample · listen
ir a + infinitive (earlier S2) what you're going to do Mañana voy a visitar el museo.
stem-changers (M2) wants, can, begins… (vowel shifts) Yo quiero ir al centro.
yo irregular (M3) what I do (special yo form) Yo salgo de casa a las ocho.

And don't forget estar from Semester 1 for location: El banco está cerca de la plaza. — here's estar in action.

✍️ PART 4 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check your verbs and spelling.

1. Say: “I'm going to turn right at the traffic light.”

Voy a doblar a la derecha en el semáforo. ir a + infinitive (voy a doblar) + the direction words.

2. Say: “I want to go downtown, and I leave the house at eight.”

Quiero ir al centro, y salgo de casa a las ocho. A stem-changer (quiero) + an irregular yo form (salgo).

3. Say: “We're going to walk straight for two blocks.”

Vamos a caminar derecho dos cuadras. ir a + infinitive in the nosotros form (vamos a) + derecho and cuadras.

🌎 CULTURA · EL METRO
Cómo se mueve la ciudad

Millions of chilangos (as residents of la Ciudad de México call themselves) get around on el metro, one of the busiest subway systems in the world. Each station has its own colorful icon — a grasshopper, a fountain, a cannon — a clever design so that everyone can navigate, whether or not they read. Knowing your directions (a la derecha, derecho, dos cuadras) still matters the moment you climb the stairs back to street level.

🇲🇽 MÉXICO A FONDO · RETRIEVAL Y ESFUERZO
El esfuerzo vale la pena

Learning to navigate a megacity like la CDMX takes patience — and so does learning its language. Retrieval practice (pulling words out of memory instead of re-reading them) feels harder than copying, and that difficulty is the point: every time you recall salgo, quiero, or voy a without looking, the pathway gets stronger. Hoy estás un paso más cerca del examen.

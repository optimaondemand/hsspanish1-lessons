IPARA EMPEZAR5 min

The rule, in one breath

Before you write anything, say the shortcut out loud: if the subject is yo, use the special form (hago, pongo, salgo, traigo, conozco, veo, doy). For anyone else, it's a plain regular verb. Hold that in your head — you're about to make the call yourself.

Build it yourself, then check

Write each sentence in Spanish, then press Check. The word key on the right has every word you need: click a word to type it into your box. If something is off, the checker tells you what to fix. The answer unlocks once you have tried.

1. Write: “I do my homework downtown.”

Yo hago mis tareas en el centro. The subject is yo → the special form hago. Then mis tareas + en el centro.

2. Write: “I'm going to turn right at the traffic light.”

Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Voy a doblar a la derecha en el semáforo. ir a + infinitive (voy a doblar) + the direction, a la derecha, + where: en el semáforo.

3. Write: “I want to go downtown, and I leave the house at eight.”

Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Quiero ir al centro, y salgo de casa a las ocho. A boot verb (quiero, e→ie) + the infinitive ir, then a special yo form (salgo).

4. Write: “We're going to walk two blocks straight.”

Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Vamos a caminar dos cuadras derecho. ir a + infinitive in the nosotros form (vamos a caminar) + dos cuadras + derecho. Vamos a caminar derecho dos cuadras is right too: derecho can go before or after.

🔑 WORD KEY

ir (to go)
  • voy a — I am going to
  • vamos a — we are going to
  • ir — to go
Verbs
  • hacer — to do, make
  • querer (ie) — to want
  • salir — to leave, go out
  • doblar — to turn
  • caminar — to walk
After voy a, vamos a and quiero, the next verb stays an infinitive. Boot verbs change inside the boot (e→ie). With yo, hacer and salir take their special forms.
Directions and places
  • a la derecha — to the right
  • derecho — straight ahead
  • el semáforo — the traffic light
  • dos cuadras — two blocks
  • al centro — to downtown
  • en el centro — in downtown
  • de casa — from home
Also useful
  • en — at, in
  • y — and
  • a las ocho — at eight
  • mis tareas — my homework
  • yo — I
  • nosotros — we
🇲🇽 MÉXICO A FONDO · RETRIEVAL Y ESFUERZO
El esfuerzo vale la pena

Learning to navigate a megacity like la CDMX takes patience — and so does learning its language. Retrieval practice (pulling words out of memory instead of re-reading them) feels harder than copying, and that difficulty is the point: every time you recall salgo, quiero, or voy a without looking, the pathway gets stronger.

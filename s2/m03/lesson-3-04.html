★ Aplicación — Use the Verbs, Train the Ear
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the pattern. Today you use it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the irregular-yo verbs, one day after the first on purpose — spacing is how the pattern becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. You'll make the choice yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation using this module's own clips. It's all ungraded practice: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose the correct form of an irregular-yo verb and explain why.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken sentence with these verbs and write down what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The rule, in one breath

Before you write anything, say the shortcut out loud: if the subject is yo, use the special form (hago, pongo, salgo, traigo, conozco, veo, doy). For anyone else, it's a plain regular verb. Hold that in your head — you're about to make the call yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
The irregular yo forms — how comfortable do you feel right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You make the choice

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank with the correct present-tense form of the verb in parentheses. Say why to yourself as you write — is the subject yo or not? Commit to all six before you reveal the key.

YO — special form

hago · pongo · salgo · traigo
conozco · veo · doy

OTHERS — regular

-es · -e · -emos / -imos · -en
haces, hace, hacemos, hacen…
  1. Yo ______ (salir) de casa temprano.
  2. Nosotros ______ (hacer) las compras en el mercado.
  3. Yo ______ (ver) el museo desde la plaza.
  4. Ella ______ (poner) el dinero en el banco.
  5. Yo ______ (dar) un paseo por el centro.
  6. Ellos ______ (conocer) la ciudad muy bien.
1. salgo — subject is yo → special form. (Yo salgo de casa temprano.)
2. hacemos — nosotros → regular. (Nosotros hacemos las compras.)
3. veo — subject is yo → special form. (Yo veo el museo.)
4. pone — ella → regular. (Ella pone el dinero en el banco.)
5. doy — subject is yo → special form. (Yo doy un paseo.)
6. conocen — ellos → regular. (Ellos conocen la ciudad.)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two more sentences about your real life — one starting with Yo (force a special form) and one about a family member (force a regular form). Read them aloud.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same clips you met on Day 2 — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your spelling, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Yo salgo de casa a las ocho. (I leave home at eight.) — salgo, the special yo form of salir.
2
Write what you hear…
Yo pongo el dinero en el banco. (I put the money in the bank.) — pongo, from poner.
3
Write what you hear…
Yo traigo un mapa de la ciudad. (I bring a map of the city.) — traigo, from traer.
4
Write what you hear…
Yo veo el museo en la plaza. (I see the museum in the plaza.) — veo, from ver.
5
Write what you hear…
Yo doy un paseo por el centro. (I take a stroll through downtown.) — doy, from dar.

Notice every sentence started with Yo — which is exactly why every verb used its special form. That's the pattern your ear is learning to expect.

🧠 PART 4 · ¿YO O NO? 8 min

Two to finish on

Same question every time: is the subject yo (special form) or someone else (regular)?

✅ TRY IT · choose the right form
“Yo ______ a mis amigos en la plaza.” (from ver)
✅ TRY IT · choose the right form
“Mi hermano ______ un mapa cuando viaja.” (from traer)

One to build yourself: how would you say "I do my homework downtown" (from hacer)? Say it, then reveal.

Yo hago mis tareas en el centro. Subject is yo → the special form hago.

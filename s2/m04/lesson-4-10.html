🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear sentences and identify the reflexive pronoun and verb form. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask: which pronoun (me, te, se, nos), and does the vowel jump?

  • Yo me levanto a las seis.yo → me · regular verb
  • ¿A qué hora te levantas?tú → te
  • Ella se viste rápido.ella → se · e→i jump
  • Nosotros nos duchamos por la mañana.nosotros → nos
  • Ellos se acuestan tarde.ellos → se · o→ue jump
  • Primero me despierto, después me ducho y me visto.three steps, in order

Hear the pattern? Every sentence puts a little pronoun right in front of its verb. That single habit — match the pronoun, jump the vowel if needed — is the whole quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · DOS ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first — it packs several reflexives into one breath.

  • Me lavo la cara y me cepillo los dientes.two reflexive actions, both me
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In «___ lavo la cara», which pronoun did you hear — and which is correct?
✅ DECIDE · does the vowel jump?
«Yo ___ a las diez.» (I go to bed at ten.) — acostarse.
✅ DECIDE · which pronoun?
«Nosotros ___ despertamos temprano.» (We wake up early.)

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

Reflexive = pronoun + verb. Match the pronoun to the subject (me, te, se, nos, se), conjugate the verb normally, and if it's despertarse / acostarse / sentarse / vestirse, jump the vowel in every form except nosotros.

📝 PART 3 · LA PRUEBA DE GRAMÁTICA 18 min

Grammar Quiz — verbos reflexivos

This is the graded quiz for Módulo 4. Before you launch it, read what to expect:

  • 30 points total · 2 attempts (your higher score counts).
  • Includes 10–15 points of listening — short clips like the ones you just heard. Have your sound on.
  • The rest asks you to choose the right pronoun and conjugate reflexive verbs, including the stem-changers (despertarse, acostarse, vestirse).
  • No new vocabulary — everything comes from this module.

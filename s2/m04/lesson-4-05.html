Lesson 4.05
★ A leer — La rutina de Camila en Quito
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
No new grammar today — just reading. You'll meet Camila and hear reflexive verbs and sequence words working together in a real little story.
This is your third encounter with the reflexive pattern — and the most relaxed. Instead of conjugating in a drill, you'll simply notice the verbs at work inside meaningful input: a short paragraph about a teenager's day in Quito, Ecuador. Read it, listen to it, and let the pattern settle. Then we'll take a cultural detour to Ecuador, where Camila lives.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to read and understand a short paragraph that narrates a daily routine with reflexive verbs.
2
I will be able to locate Ecuador and Quito and name one thing the country is known for.
⏰ PART 1 · ANTES DE LEER 8 min

Before you read

Good readers guess before they read. Camila is a teenager in Quito, the capital of Ecuador, high in the Andes. As you go into the paragraph, keep one question in mind: what does she do first (primero), and what does she do after (después)? You're tracking the order of a day.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Think about your own morning. Which comes first for you?
📚 PART 2 · LA LECTURA · Camila 16 min

Listen first, then read

Click 🔊 to hear the whole paragraph read aloud by a native speaker. Listen once with your eyes closed, then read along a second time. Don't worry about every word — you already know the important ones.

Escuchar la lectura completa — La rutina de Camila

«Hola, me llamo Camila. Soy de Quito, Ecuador, la ciudad en la mitad del mundo. Entre semana me despierto temprano, a las seis, con el despertador. Primero me levanto y me ducho. Después me visto y me cepillo los dientes. Mi hermana se peina y se pone el uniforme. Desayunamos juntas y vamos a la escuela. Por la noche me acuesto tarde porque estudio mucho. Los fines de semana no me levanto temprano; ¡me quedo en la cama!»

Not sure of a word? Reveal a mini-glossary — but try to read without it first.

me llamo = my name is · la mitad del mundo = the middle of the world (the equator) · entre semana = on weekdays · temprano = early · el despertador = the alarm clock · los dientes = teeth · el uniforme = the uniform · desayunamos = we eat breakfast · juntos = together · por la noche = at night · tarde = late · los fines de semana = on weekends · me quedo en la cama = I stay in bed
✅ PART 3 · ¿COMPRENDISTE? 16 min

Check your understanding

Answer from the paragraph. These are reading questions — you're showing you understood, not conjugating a verb.

✅ TRY IT · from the reading
¿A qué hora se despierta Camila entre semana? (What time does Camila wake up on weekdays?)
✅ TRY IT · from the reading
¿Qué hace Camila primero? (What does Camila do first?)
✅ TRY IT · from the reading
¿Qué hace Camila los fines de semana? (What does Camila do on weekends?)

In your own words: why does Camila se acuesta tarde during the week? Say it out loud, then reveal.

Because she estudia mucho — she studies a lot. So she wakes up early (se despierta temprano) but goes to bed late (se acuesta tarde). Two reflexives, opposite ends of her day.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Go back to the paragraph and be a detective. Find every reflexive verb (there are many: me despierto, me levanto, me ducho, me visto, me cepillo, se peina, se pone, me acuesto, me quedo). For each one, whisper who does it and whether the vowel jumps. This is the same skill the Day-10 quiz will ask for — but here it's just noticing.
🌎 CULTURA · ECUADOR
Quito, la mitad del mundo

Camila lives in Quito, the capital of Ecuador and one of the highest capital cities in the world (about 2,850 meters up in the Andes). Its old center is so beautifully preserved that it was one of the very first places named a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Just north of the city, a yellow line marks La Mitad del Mundo — the equator — where visitors take a photo with a foot in each hemisphere.

Because Ecuador sits right on the equator, the sun comes up and goes down at nearly the same time every day of the year. That steady light shapes daily life: routines like Camila's stay remarkably consistent from January to July — there's no long dark winter to sleep through.

🌎 CULTURA A FONDO · ECUADOR
El desayuno ecuatoriano

After Camila se viste, her family sits down to breakfast (el desayuno). In the Ecuadorian highlands that often means humitas (steamed fresh-corn cakes wrapped in husks) or bolones de verde (fried green-plantain balls with cheese), with café or hot colada. Corn and the plantain have been staples of Andean tables for thousands of years — a daily routine that connects a teenager in Quito to a very long history.

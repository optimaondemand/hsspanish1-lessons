TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the system. Today you use it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the full reflexive system, and it lands a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how the pattern becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. Instead you'll build your own routine sentences in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation. It's all ungraded practice: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built. You'll close with a short spiral that lines the stem-changing reflexives up with the Módulo 2 stem-changers.

IPARA EMPEZAR5 min

The rule, in one breath

Before you write anything, say the recipe out loud: pronoun that matches the subject (me, te, se, nos) + verb with its normal ending — and if it's despertarse / acostarse / sentarse / vestirse, jump the vowel in every form except nosotros. Hold that in your head — you're about to build sentences yourself.

IIESCRITURA GUIADA14 min

You build the sentence

Type the correct pronoun + verb in each box. Think why as you type — which pronoun, and does the vowel jump? Commit to all six before you reveal the key; the thinking is the practice.

The pronoun

yo → me · tú → te
él/ella/usted → se
nosotros → nos
ellos/ustedes → se

Vowel jumps (not in nosotros)

despertarse → me despierto
acostarse → me acuesto
sentarse → me siento
vestirse → me visto
  1. Yo ______ (levantarse) a las siete.
  2. Mi hermana ______ (ducharse) por la mañana.
  3. Yo ______ (despertarse) muy temprano.
  4. Nosotros ______ (acostarse) a las diez.
  5. Tú ______ (cepillarse) los dientes.
  6. Ellos ______ (vestirse) rápido.
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
1. me levanto — regular; pronoun me. (Yo me levanto a las siete.)
2. se ducha — regular; pronoun se for ella. (Mi hermana se ducha.)
3. me despierto — e→ie jump. (Yo me despierto muy temprano.)
4. nos acostamos — nosotros, so NO vowel jump. (Nosotros nos acostamos.)
5. te cepillas — regular; pronoun te. (Tú te cepillas los dientes.)
6. se visten — e→i jump. (Ellos se visten rápido.)
IIIDICTADO13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same reflexive sentences you met yesterday — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind your accent marks, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
1
Write what you hear…
Yo me levanto a las seis. (I get up at six.) — pronoun me + regular verb.
2
Write what you hear…
¿A qué hora te levantas? (What time do you get up?) — note the opening ¿ and the accents on ¿A qué hora?
3
Write what you hear…
Ella se viste rápido. (She gets dressed quickly.) — the e→i form viste; accent on rápido.
4
Write what you hear…
Nosotros nos duchamos por la mañana. (We shower in the morning.) — nosotros pronoun nos; watch the ñ in mañana.
5
Write what you hear…
Ellos se acuestan tarde. (They go to bed late.) — the o→ue form acuestan; pronoun se.

Notice every sentence had a pronoun sitting right in front of its verb — that's the shape your ear is learning to expect.

VESPIRAL · EL CAMBIO DE RAÍZ10 min

The same vowel jump, twice over

Your stem-changing reflexives aren't new magic — they reuse the exact jump you learned in Módulo 2. Listen across each row and hear the family resemblance:

Módulo 2 verb · listenSame changeMódulo 4 reflexive
quiero e→ie me despierto
empieza e→ie me siento
duerme o→ue me acuesto
pido e→i me visto

And remember the exception both families share: nosotros never jumps. Queremos, nos acostamos — back to the plain vowel.

✅ SPIRAL CHECK · does the vowel jump?
"Yo ___ a las diez." (I go to bed at ten.) — acostarse, o→ue.
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · the nosotros trap
"Nosotros ___ temprano los lunes." (We wake up early on Mondays.)
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · does the vowel jump?
"Ella ___ para la escuela." (She gets dressed for school.) — vestirse, e→i.

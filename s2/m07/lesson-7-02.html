Lesson 7.02
★ Experiencias — y el pretérito de ir
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A few experience words — and the single most useful past-tense verb you'll learn all module.
First, Set B: the words for what you do and bring back from a trip. Then we meet your first irregular preterite: the verb ir (to go) — fui, fuiste, fue… Here's the two-for-one surprise: ser (to be) has the exact same preterite forms. So fui can mean “I went” or “I was,” and context makes it clear. Learn one set of forms, get two verbs. We'll do the other irregulars tomorrow — today, just this one.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use travel-experience words: la foto, el recuerdo, la excursión, explorar, quedarse, la aventura.
2
I will be able to say where I went in the past with the preterite of ir (fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fueron).
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

A quick look back

In Módulo 6 you learned to say what you did with regular verbs: Ayer viajé (yesterday I traveled), visité el museo (I visited the museum). Today's verb is different — it doesn't follow the pattern. It's worth memorizing on its own because you'll use it constantly.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Think of the last place you went — even just yesterday. Where was it?
📚 PART 2 · EXPERIENCIAS (SET B) 11 min

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Pair each Set B word with a Set A word from yesterday: una foto de la montaña, una excursión a las ruinas, un recuerdo del pueblo. Say three of your own aloud.
🧠 PART 3 · EL PRETÉRITO DE IR (= SER) 12 min

One verb, learned by heart

The preterite of ir (to go) doesn't follow the regular -ar / -er / -ir endings you learned in Módulo 6 — you just memorize it. The reward: these same forms are also the preterite of ser (to be). Listen and repeat each one:

Subjectir / ser (preterite)Example · listen
yofuiAyer fui a Machu Picchu.
túfuiste¿Adónde fuiste de vacaciones?
usted / él / ellafueEl viaje fue increíble.
nosotros/asfuimosFuimos al aeropuerto.
ustedes / ellos / ellasfueronEllos fueron al pueblo.

Two things to notice. (1) There are no accent marks anywhere — not on fui, not on fue. (2) Fue is both “he/she went” and “it was.” El viaje fue increíble = “The trip was amazing” (that's ser). Ella fue a Perú = “She went to Peru” (that's ir). Context tells you which.

✅ TRY IT · choose the form
"Yo ___ al aeropuerto ayer." (I ___ to the airport yesterday.)
✅ TRY IT · choose the form
"¿Adónde ___ (tú) el verano pasado?" (Where did you go last summer?)

Your turn: how would you say "We went to the mountain"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Fuimos a la montaña. "We" → fuimos; and a la for “to the.” No accent mark needed.

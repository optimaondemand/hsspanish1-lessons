★ Perú en el mapa — y un viaje por el mundo hispano

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A breather between two big days — explore, don't cram.
Yesterday you recorded (Tarea oral); tomorrow you take the Grammar Quiz. Today sits between them on purpose. Nothing here is graded. First you'll find Perú and los Andes on the interactive map — Lima, Cusco, and the mountains behind Andrea's story. Then you'll preview this semester's VR project in ENGAGE, Un viaje por el mundo hispano, where you'll travel a Spanish-speaking destination and narrate the trip you took — in the past tense. Today is preview and prep.

🌎 CULTURA · LOS PAÍSES ANDINOS
Una cordillera, muchos países

Los Andes cruzan siete países hispanohablantes o vecinos: Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Perú, Bolivia, Chile y Argentina. En Bolivia está La Paz, la capital más alta del mundo (a más de 3.600 metros); en Ecuador, la línea del ecuador da nombre al país. Cada país andino comparte las montañas — y una historia que empezó mucho antes de los mapas modernos.

🥾 PART 3 · PROYECTO DE VR · ENGAGE 14 min

Un viaje por el mundo hispano — your semester VR project

This semester's big VR project puts on a headset and drops you into ENGAGE to travel the Spanish-speaking world. You'll choose a destination — the Peruvian Andes, a Caribbean beach, a Mexican city — move through its locations, and then narrate the trip you took, entirely in the past tense. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE PROJECT
  1. Elegir un destino — choose a place in the Spanish-speaking world and enter its VR location.
  2. Explorar — explore the scene: the airport, the market, the mountains, the sea. Collect details you'll describe later.
  3. Narrar en el pasado — narrate your trip using the preterite: Fui a…, hice…, tuve…, estuve…, visité…, compré…
  4. Compartir — share your recorded narration; your teacher assembles the class's trips into a gallery walk through the whole Spanish-speaking world.

Rehearse your opening line

💭 REHEARSE · no wrong answer — try it out
You step into your VR destination. How do you begin narrating your trip?

Build one sentence before you ever step in

You explored a market on your trip and bought a souvenir. How would you say "I explored the market and I bought a souvenir" — using a regular then an irregular idea? Say it aloud, then reveal.

Exploré el mercado y compré un recuerdo. Both regular preterite here (-é); pair them with an irregular like Fui al mercado to open. This is exactly the narration the VR project asks for.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Sketch a quick “trip script” for yourself: pick a destination and write five past-tense lines — where you went (fui), what you did (hice), what you had (tuve), where you were (estuve), and one more with a regular verb (visité, exploré, compré). Keep it; you'll walk into the VR project with your narration ready.
🌎 CULTURA · A FONDO · EL TURISMO RESPONSABLE
Viajar con respeto

Machu Picchu now limits daily visitors to protect the fragile ruins, and many Andean communities ask travelers to buy directly from local artisans and to greet elders with usted. Being a good turista means more than taking photos — it means treating a place, and its people, with care. When you narrate your VR trip, notice not just what you did but how you traveled.

★ Aplicación — Write Your Trip, Train Your Ear
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You've met the forms. Today you use them — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the irregular preterite, landing a day after the first on purpose — spacing is how the forms become automatic. There's no new grammar today. Instead you'll fill in the right past-tense verb in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-sentence dictation of the exact model sentences you heard yesterday. It's all ungraded practice: mistakes here are how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to complete sentences with the correct irregular preterite form and explain my choice.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken preterite sentence and write down exactly what I hear.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The pattern, in one breath

Before you write, say the four stems out loud: ir/ser → fui, hacer → hice, tener → tuve, estar → estuve. Then the endings: -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -ieron, with no accents on yo and él/ella. Hold that in your head — you're about to make the calls yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Yesterday's four verbs — how comfortable do you feel right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 14 min

You make the choice

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and fill the blank with the correct preterite form of the verb in parentheses. Say why to yourself as you write (which verb? who did it?). Commit to all six before you reveal the key — the thinking is the practice.

THE STEMS

ir/ser → fu- · hacer → hic-
tener → tuv- · estar → estuv-

THE ENDINGS

yo -e · tú -iste · él/ella -o
nosotros -imos · ellos -ieron
  1. El verano pasado yo ______ (ir) a Perú.
  2. Nosotros ______ (hacer) una excursión a Machu Picchu.
  3. El viaje ______ (ser) increíble.
  4. Mi hermano ______ (tener) un problema con su pasaporte.
  5. Yo ______ (estar) en las montañas una semana.
  6. ¿Adónde ______ (ir) tú de vacaciones?
1. fui — yo, ir/ser → fu- + -e, no accent.
2. hicimos — nosotros, hacer → hic- + -imos.
3. fue — el viaje (él), ser → fu- + -o = fue. “The trip was.”
4. tuvo — mi hermano (él), tener → tuv- + -o, no accent.
5. estuve — yo, estar → estuv- + -e, no accent.
6. fuiste — tú, ir → fu- + -iste.
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write three more sentences of your own about a real or imagined trip — one with fui, one with hice, and one with estuve. Read them aloud. Keep them; you'll reuse them for the Tarea oral on Day 8.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 13 min

Listen and write — five sentences

These are the same model sentences you met yesterday — now the training wheels are off. For each: click 🔊 (replay as often as you need), write down exactly what you hear, watch the accent marks on the vocabulary (not the verbs!), then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write what you hear…
Ayer fui a Machu Picchu. (Yesterday I went to Machu Picchu.) — fui, no accent.
2
Write what you hear…
El viaje fue increíble. (The trip was amazing.) — fue = “was” here (ser); note the accent on increíble.
3
Write what you hear…
Hice muchas fotos en las ruinas. (I took many photos in the ruins.) — hice, no accent.
4
Write what you hear…
Tuve un día maravilloso. (I had a wonderful day.) — tuve; note the accent on día.
5
Write what you hear…
Estuve en las montañas una semana. (I was in the mountains for a week.) — estuve, no accent; watch the ñ in montañas.

Notice the verbs themselves carry no accent marks — fui, fue, hice, tuve, estuve. The accents you hear land on the travel words around them. That's the pattern your ear is learning.

🧠 PART 4 · ¿QUÉ FORMA? 8 min

Two to finish on

Same questions every time: which verb, and who did it?

✅ TRY IT · choose the form
"Nosotros ___ una aventura en los Andes." (We had an adventure in the Andes.)
✅ TRY IT · choose the form
"Ellos ___ fotos del mar." (They took photos of the sea.)

One to build yourself: how would you say "I was at the hotel"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Estuve en el hotel. estar → estuv- + -e = estuve; “in the hotel” → en el hotel.

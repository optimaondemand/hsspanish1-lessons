🧠 PART 3 · ESPIRAL · REGULAR + IRREGULAR 11 min

Two kinds of past, one story

In Módulo 6 you learned regular preterite verbs — they keep the accents (viajé, visité, exploré, compré). This module's irregular verbs drop them (fui, hice, tuve, estuve). A real trip story uses both. Read across the row and hear them together:

Type (from)FormsExample
regular (Módulo 6) viajé · visité · exploré · compré Viajé a Perú y exploré un pueblo.
irregular (Módulo 7) fui · hice · tuve · estuve Fui a las ruinas e hice muchas fotos.

Hear all of them collide in one sentence: Fui a Perú, hice una excursión y estuve muy contento.

Present vs. preterite — today or already done?

PRESENTE — happens now / usually

Voy a Perú. (I go / am going.)
Hago fotos. (I take photos.)
Estoy en casa. (I am at home.)

PRETÉRITO — already happened

Fui a Perú. (I went.)
Hice fotos. (I took photos.)
Estuve en casa. (I was at home.)
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · present or past?
"Ayer ___ una excursión a las ruinas." (Ayer = yesterday, so it's past.)
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · regular or irregular?
"El verano pasado yo ___ a Colombia." (I traveled… — a regular -ar verb, viajar.)
✅ SPIRAL CHECK · choose the past form
"Nosotros ___ un día maravilloso en el mar." (We had… — irregular tener.)
✍️ PART 4 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check your verb choice and spelling.

1. Say: “Last summer I traveled to Peru and I went to the ruins.”

El verano pasado viajé a Perú y fui a las ruinas. viajé = regular (keeps accent); fui = irregular (no accent).

2. Say: “We took a lot of photos and we had an adventure.”

Hicimos muchas fotos y tuvimos una aventura. Both irregular: hic- + -imos, tuv- + -imos.

3. Say: “I explored a town and I was in the mountains.”

Exploré un pueblo y estuve en las montañas. exploré = regular (accent); estuve = irregular (no accent).
🌎 CULTURA · LA MÚSICA ANDINA
El sonido de los Andes

In the markets and plazas of Cusco you'll hear the bright, breathy sound of the quena (a bamboo flute) and the small guitar-like charango. The most famous Andean melody, «El cóndor pasa», was written in Perú in 1913 and later traveled the world. That music — like the preterite — carries the past into the present.

🌎 CULTURA · A FONDO · TEXTILES Y LANA
El recuerdo de Andrea

Remember the recuerdo Andrea bought — un gorro de lana, a wool hat? Andean weaving is thousands of years old. The wool comes from the alpaca and llama, animals the Inca raised high in the mountains, and the bright geometric patterns often encode a weaver's home village. Buying a handwoven recuerdo in a Peruvian mercado supports families keeping that craft alive.

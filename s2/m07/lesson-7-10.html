🔊 PART 1 · CALIENTA EL OÍDO 10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear sentences and identify the preterite verb. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask: which verb is this (ir/ser, hacer, tener, estar) and who did it?

  • Ayer fui a Machu Picchu.ir → I went
  • El viaje fue increíble.ser → it was
  • Hice muchas fotos en las ruinas.hacer → I did/made
  • Tuve un día maravilloso.tener → I had
  • Estuve en las montañas una semana.estar → I was
  • ¿Adónde fuiste de vacaciones?ir (tú) → where did you go?

Hear the pattern? Every one of these carries no accent mark on the verb, and the stress sits on the stem. That's the whole quiz.

🧠 PART 2 · TRES ENSAYOS 8 min

Practice runs — exactly the quiz format

Listen to this one first — three irregulars in a single sentence, the trickiest kind.

  • Fui a Perú, hice una excursión y estuve muy contento.fui + hice + estuve, one breath
🔊 LISTEN & DECIDE · play the clip above again
In «___ una excursión», which verb did you hear — and which is correct?
✅ DECIDE · which form?
«Nosotros ___ en el hotel dos noches.» (We were in the hotel two nights.)
✅ DECIDE · present or preterite?
«El año pasado ___ a Bolivia.» (Last year I went to Bolivia.)

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

Stems: ir/ser → fu-, hacer → hic-, tener → tuv-, estar → estuv-. Endings: -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -ieron, with no accents on yo and él/ella. And hacer's él form is hizo (with a z).

Grammar Quiz — irregular preterite

This is the graded quiz for Módulo 7. Before you launch it, read what to expect:

  • 30 points total · 2 attempts (your higher score counts).
  • Includes 10–15 points of listening — short clips like the ones you just heard. Have your sound on.
  • The rest asks you to choose or type the correct preterite form of ir/ser, hacer, tener, and estar — and to tell present from past.
  • No new vocabulary — everything comes from this module and Módulo 6's regular preterite.

Lesson 7.05 · Week 2 begins
★ El viaje de Andrea — A Trip Through los Andes
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Your third encounter with the irregular preterite — this time inside a real story.
Grammar sticks best when it's carrying meaning. Today you'll read (and hear) Andrea tell the story of her family's trip to Perú — the airport, the flight to Cusco, the excursion to Machu Picchu. Every past-tense verb you've learned in this module is in there, doing its job. Then you'll tell a trip of your own in a few past-tense sentences, and a culture spotlight takes you deeper into Perú and its food. No new grammar — just the forms alive in a paragraph.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to read a short first-person travel story and understand the main events.
2
I will be able to spot the irregular preterite verbs in context and say what each one means.
3
I will be able to say a short trip story that mixes regular and irregular preterite verbs.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

Before you read

You're about to read a whole trip in the past tense. As you read, do one thing: every time you see a verb, ask “who did this, and when?” The -e and -o endings with no accent are your signal that it already happened.

📖 PART 2 · A LEER Y ESCUCHAR 14 min

El viaje de Andrea

Press play, follow along, then read it again on your own. The irregular preterite verbs are in bold.

Play the reading

Glosario: antigua (old / ancient) · impresionantes (impressive) · diferente (different) · pequeño (small) · un problema (a problem) · la altura (the altitude) · inolvidable (unforgettable)

«Hola, me llamo Andrea. El verano pasado fui a Perú con mi familia. Primero, llegamos al aeropuerto de Lima con nuestros pasaportes. Después, tomamos un avión a Cusco, una ciudad antigua en las montañas. Con un guía, hice una excursión a Machu Picchu. ¡Las ruinas fueron impresionantes! Estuve en los Andes una semana. Cada día hice muchas fotos y exploré un pueblo diferente. Mi hermana tuvo un pequeño problema con la altura, pero estuvo bien. Compré un recuerdo — un gorro de lana (a wool hat) — y tuve una aventura inolvidable. El viaje fue increíble.»

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Point to each bold verb and say its infinitive and meaning: fui (ir — I went), hice (hacer — I did/made), fueron (ser — they were), estuve (estar — I was), tuvo (tener — she had), estuvo (estar — she was), tuve (tener — I had), fue (ser — it was). (Hice appears twice.) Notice not one of them carries an accent mark.

✍️ PART 5 · A PRODUCIR 8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check your verb choice and spelling.

1. Say: “Last summer I traveled to Peru and I went to the ruins.”

El verano pasado viajé a Perú y fui a las ruinas. viajé = regular (keeps accent); fui = irregular (no accent).

2. Say: “We took a lot of photos and we had an adventure.”

Hicimos muchas fotos y tuvimos una aventura. Both irregular: hic- + -imos, tuv- + -imos.

3. Say: “I explored a town and I was in the mountains.”

Exploré un pueblo y estuve en las montañas. exploré = regular (accent); estuve = irregular (no accent).
🌎 CULTURA · PERÚ
Del mar a la montaña

Perú es un país de contrastes (contrasts): la capital, Lima, está en la costa del océano Pacífico; Cusco y Machu Picchu están en los Andes; y al este (to the east) está la selva amazónica (the Amazon rainforest). A traveler like Andrea can move from el mar to la montaña in a single trip. Cusco sits at about 3.400 meters — high enough that visitors sip mate de coca (coca-leaf tea) to ease la altura, the altitude Andrea's sister struggled with.

🌎 CULTURA · A FONDO · LA COCINA PERUANA
Una de las mejores del mundo (One of the best in the world)

Peruvian food is celebrated worldwide, and it grew from the same meeting of cultures as its language. The star dish is ceviche — fresh fish “cooked” in lime juice, from the Pacific coast. Lomo saltado stir-fries beef with soy sauce, a gift of Chinese-Peruvian (chifa) cooking. And the humble papa (potato) was first domesticated in the Peruvian Andes — Perú grows over 3,000 varieties. When you eat in a Peruvian restaurant or at a mercado (market) stall, you're tasting centuries of history.

🌎 CULTURA · A FONDO · TEXTILES Y LANA
El recuerdo de Andrea

Remember the recuerdo Andrea bought — un gorro de lana, a wool hat? Andean weaving is thousands of years old. The wool comes from the alpaca and llama, animals the Inca raised high in the mountains, and the bright geometric patterns often encode a weaver's home village. Buying a handwoven recuerdo in a Peruvian mercado supports families keeping that craft alive.

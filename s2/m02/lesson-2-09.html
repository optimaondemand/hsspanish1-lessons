🌎 CULTURA · EL CONO SUR
Cuatro países, una región

The Cono Sur gathers Chile (Santiago), Argentina (Buenos Aires), Uruguay (Montevideo), and Paraguay (Asunción). Across the region people share the mate tea habit, a love of asado (barbecue), and, in the far south, the vast wilderness of Patagonia. Remember the seasons are flipped: while the northern hemisphere has summer, the Cono Sur is in winter.

🥾 PART 3 · VISTA PREVIA DE LA VR · ENGAGE 14 min

Un viaje por el mundo hispano — your semester VR project

Later this semester you'll put on a headset and step into ENGAGE, our VR platform, and arrive in a Spanish-speaking city during a festival. You'll find your way through the streets, check into a place to stay, order food at a café or market — and afterward you'll recount what you did. You're not performing today. This is your preview, so that when the session comes you already know the moves.

🎓 WHAT YOU'LL DO IN THE PROJECT
  1. Llegar — arrive in the city during a festival. Take in the plaza, the music, the crowds — and greet people warmly.
  2. Orientarte — find your way. Ask for and follow directions to get around (you'll build this vocabulary in Módulo 3, la ciudad).
  3. El alojamiento — check into a place to stay. A hostel or hotel: say what you want and what you need (quiero una habitación…).
  4. Pedir comida — order food. At a café or market stall (pido… / quiero…).
  5. Recontar — recount what you did. Back “home,” tell the story of your day — where you went, what you ate, what you saw.

Notice how much of this you already own: the boot verbs querer, poder, and pedir are exactly the ones you'll use to ask for a room and order food.

Rehearse one line before you ever step in

At the front desk you want to say “I want a room, please.” Using querer + habitación, how would you say it? Say it aloud, then reveal.

Quiero una habitación, por favor. Boot form quiero (e→ie) + una habitación (a room) — the same vocabulary and verb from this module, now doing travel work.

🏔 CULTURA A FONDO · CHILE
Las Fiestas Patrias y la cueca

Chile's biggest festival is Fiestas Patrias, around September 18. Families set up fondas and ramadas (festive stalls), grill asado, fly kites (volantines), and dance the cueca, the national dance, waving white handkerchiefs. If your VR journey lands you in Santiago in September, this is the festival you'd walk into — music, food, and a whole city celebrating. ¡Viva Chile!

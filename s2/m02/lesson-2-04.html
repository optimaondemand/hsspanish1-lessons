IPARA EMPEZAR5 min

The rule, in one breath

Before you write anything, say it out loud: the stem vowel changes in every form except nosotros. Inside the boot → change (quiero, puedes, duerme, juega, pido). Outside the boot (nosotros) → no change (queremos, podemos, dormimos).

IIESCRITURA GUIADA14 min

You make the choice

Type into the box after each sentence to fill the blank with the correct present-tense form of the verb in parentheses. Say why to yourself — boot form or nosotros? Commit to all six before you reveal the key.

INSIDE THE BOOT — changes

yo · tú · él/ella/usted · ellos/ellas/ustedes
quiero · puedes · duerme · juegan

OUTSIDE — no change

nosotros / nosotras only
queremos · podemos · dormimos · jugamos

  2. Nosotros ______ (poder) limpiar el baño.
  3. Mi hermana ______ (dormir) en el dormitorio de arriba.
  4. Los niños ______ (jugar) en el jardín.
  5. El día ______ (empezar) en la cocina.
  6. Yo ______ (pedir) ayuda (help) con los quehaceres.

1. quiero — boot form (e→ie). (Yo quiero una casa con jardín.)
2. podemos — nosotros, outside the boot → no change. (Not “puedemos.”)
3. duerme — boot form (o→ue), él/ella.
4. juegan — boot form (u→ue), ellos.
5. empieza — boot form (e→ie), the day “begins.”
6. pido — boot form (e→i), yo.

VA PRODUCIR8 min

Build it yourself, then check

For each prompt, say the full Spanish sentence out loud first — commit before you peek. Then reveal to check the verb form and spelling.

1. Say: “I study at home, but I want to sleep.”

Yo estudio en casa, pero quiero dormir. Regular estudio (no change) + boot verb quiero (e→ie) + the infinitive dormir.

2. Say: “My sister cleans the bathroom and tidies her room.”

Mi hermana limpia el baño y ordena su habitación. Both limpiar and ordenar are regular — no stem change: limpia, ordena.

3. Say: “We want to tidy the living room.” (watch the nosotros!)

Nosotros queremos ordenar la sala. Nosotros → queremos keeps its stem (no change), then the infinitive ordenar.
🌎 CULTURA · CHILE
La once chilena

Around five or six in the evening, many Chilean families gather for la once — an afternoon “tea” with bread (pan), avocado, cheese, ham, and tea or coffee. It's a relaxed moment at the kitchen or dining table, often more social than dinner. Nosotros queremos tomar once juntos — “we want to have once together” — and notice: queremos, the nosotros form, keeps its stem.

🌎 CULTURA · CHILE
Los quehaceres en familia

In many Chilean households, weekend mornings mean everyone pitching in: one person limpia the kitchen, another ordena the bedrooms, someone sweeps the patio. Chores are often framed as a shared family effort — nosotros ordenamos la casa juntos — rather than a solo task. Sharing the work is part of how a household runs.

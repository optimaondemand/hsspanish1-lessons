ICALIENTA EL OÍDO10 min

Tune your ear before the quiz

Part of today's quiz is listening — you'll hear sentences and decide the correct verb form. Warm up first. Click each 🔊, listen twice, and say it back. As you listen, ask the one question that matters: did the stem vowel change (a boot form), or is this a nosotros that stayed regular?

  • Yo quiero una casa con jardín.querer, e→ie → boot form
  • ¿Puedes limpiar la cocina?poder, o→ue → boot form
  • Mi hermano duerme mucho.dormir, o→ue → boot form
  • El día empieza en la cocina.empezar, e→ie → boot form
  • Ella juega en el jardín.jugar, u→ue → boot form
  • Yo pido permiso para entrar.pedir, e→i → boot form

Hear the difference? Every one of these changed its stem. Now hear the exception below — the nosotros form that does not.

One-line memory hook before you begin. Say it, then reveal to check.

The stem vowel changes in every form except nosotros. Four patterns: e→ie (quiero), o→ue (puedo, duermo), u→ue (juego), e→i (pido). If you see “we,” the stem does not change.

De la casa al mundo

You began this module with la casa — and Chile shows how far a home can reach. Neruda turned his houses into poems; Gabriela Mistral carried a rural schoolhouse to a Nobel stage; families across the Cono Sur gather for la once and Fiestas Patrias. A home is where language starts — and where a whole culture lives. You can now describe yours in Spanish. ¡Bien hecho! (Well done!)

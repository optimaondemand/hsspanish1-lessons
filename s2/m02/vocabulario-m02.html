📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the 18 words of Módulo 2 — the same words you'll meet on the listening-based Vocabulary Check (Day 7). Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 button and say each word back out loud before you flip. If you can hear the word and picture its meaning without reading, you're ready.

Tip: nouns are shown with their article (la, el) — learn each one glued to its article. Remember el dormitorio and la habitación both mean a bedroom.

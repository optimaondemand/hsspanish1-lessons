📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the 19 words of Módulo 1 — the same words you'll meet on your listening-based checkpoint. Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 button and say each word back out loud before you flip. If you can hear the word and picture its meaning without reading, you're ready.

Tip: describing words that end in -o/-a agree with the person — a boy is alto, a girl is alta. Some (inteligente, amable, grande, joven, estadounidense) stay the same for everyone. Nouns are shown with their article (el, la) — learn each one glued to its article.

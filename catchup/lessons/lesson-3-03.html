Lesson 3.03
★ Ser vs. Estar — Two Ways to "Be"
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Spanish has two verbs for "to be" — and today you learn when to use each one.
In Module 1 you met ser (who you are, where you're from). Yesterday you learned estar (where things are, how you feel). Today the big idea clicks into place: ser tells us who/what something is; estar tells us where it is and how it's doing right now. English hides this behind one word ("is"); Spanish makes you choose — and choosing well is one of the most useful skills in the whole language.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose between ser and estar for identity, location, and feelings.
2
I will be able to describe where I am and how I feel using estar + feeling words.

(In Latin America, ustedes covers "you all" in every setting. Vosotros — Spain's informal "you all" — you'll meet later, labeled as European.)

🧠 PART 4 · SER O ESTAR? 10 min

The one rule that matters

SER — who / what something is

Identity, origin, nationality, and lasting traits.

Yo soy estudiante. (I am a student.)
Soy de Cuba. (I'm from Cuba.)
Ella es inteligente. (She is smart.)

ESTAR — where it is / how it feels

Location and right-now conditions or feelings.

Yo estoy en la escuela. (I'm at school.)
Estoy contento. (I'm happy.)
Ella está cansada. (She is tired.)

The shortcut: if you're saying who/what someone is → ser. If you're saying where they are or how they feel right now → estar.

✅ TRY IT · choose the right verb
"Mi amigo ___ en el trabajo." (My friend ___ at work.)
✅ TRY IT · choose the right verb
"María ___ de la República Dominicana." (María ___ from the Dominican Republic.)

Your turn: how would you say "We are happy"? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

Estamos contentos/as. A feeling → estar; "we" → estamos; and the adjective agrees with the group.
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

«Hola, me llamo Sofía. Soy de La Habana, Cuba. Ahora estoy en Miami con mi familia. Hoy estoy muy contenta porque no hay clases. Mi hermano está cansado, pero mi madre está ocupada en casa. Nosotros somos cubanos, y siempre estamos juntos.»

New words in this reading: ahora = now · con = with · hoy = today · porque = because · no hay clases = there's no school · mi hermano = my brother · mi madre = my mother · pero = but · siempre = always · juntos = together

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Find every ser (blue idea: who they are) and every estar (green idea: where/how). Why is it somos cubanos but estamos juntos? (Identity vs. a right-now situation.)
🌎 CULTURA · EL CARIBE
Cuba y la República Dominicana

La Habana es la capital de Cuba y está en la costa — a perfect ser/estar pair (identity vs. location). Both islands sit in the Caribbean, where Spanish blends with African and Taíno roots in music like son and bachata.

★ LA VIRTUD · PERSEVERANCE

Frida Kahlo (México) painted through a lifetime of physical pain after a devastating accident, turning suffering into some of the most recognized art in the world. Ella es una artista famosa; sus cuadros están en museos por todo el mundo. — perseverance, and one more ser/estar pair.

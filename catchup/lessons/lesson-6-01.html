📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Módulo 6. The big question this module answers is: ¿Qué haces en tu tiempo libre? — What do you do in your free time?
Today we start with the easy half: the words for fun — free time, sports, movies, the weekend, the park, a party. There's no new grammar today on purpose: we're stocking up on the nouns so that tomorrow, when a whole new family of verbs arrives (the -er and -ir verbs), it has real things to talk about.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name common free-time things and places in Spanish.
2
I will be able to talk about el fin de semana and where I go for fun.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Think about your weekend

Before any Spanish, picture a free Saturday — no school, no work. What do you actually do? Watch something, play a sport, meet up with friends, just rest? In this module you'll be able to talk about all of it. Let's start by naming the fun.

📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the key sets of Módulo 0 — the same material you'll meet on your listening-based Module 0 Checkpoint. Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 button and say each one back out loud before you flip. If you can hear it and know it without reading, you're ready.

Tip: tapping the 🔊 plays the audio and does not flip the card — so you can listen as many times as you like before checking the answer.

★ ¿Dónde? — Places & Where Things Are
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Módulo 3. This module is about where you are and how you feel.
Today we start with the easy half: places. You'll learn a handful of everyday locations — home, school, work, the city — plus the little words that say here, there, near, and far. There's no new grammar today on purpose: we're stocking up on words so that in the next lesson, when the verb estar arrives, it has something to stand on.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name common places in Spanish.
2
I will be able to use aquí, allí, cerca de, and lejos de to say where something is.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Think about your day

Before any Spanish, picture the places you move through on a normal day — where you wake up, where you learn, where the people in your family go. In this module you'll be able to talk about all of them. Let's start with the one where you spend the most time.

★ ¿Qué hay en la escuela? — School Things
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Módulo 2. The big question for this module: What do I do at school every day?
We start with the easy half: the things in a classroom — a book, a backpack, an exam, a schedule. There is no new grammar today on purpose: you're stocking up on nouns so that tomorrow, when the school verbs arrive (study, listen, practice…), they have something to act on. Learn each word with its article (el, la) — that's the gender you met in Módulo 0.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name common school objects and people in Spanish, with the correct article.
2
I will be able to recognize these words by ear when I hear them spoken.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

Picture your school day

Before any Spanish, picture where you learn — the desk, the screen, the bag you carry, the classes on your schedule. Everyone's school looks a little different, but the things are surprisingly similar. Today you'll learn to name them.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer

📚 PART 2 · LOS OBJETOS DE LA CLASE 14 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 and say each word back out loud. Notice the article in front (el, la) — learn each thing with its article so the gender sticks.

  • el maestro / la maestrateacher
  • la mochilabackpack

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Look around wherever you study and point at what you can name in Spanish: el libro, la mochila, el horario… Say each one out loud with its article. Anything you can't name yet, jot it down — you'll pick up more words all module.

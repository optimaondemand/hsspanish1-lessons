📖 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
These are the describing words of Módulo 7 — the appearance and personality words you'll meet on your listening-based checkpoint, plus the Módulo 1 traits you've now made systematic. Each card shows the Spanish; tap the card to flip it and check the English. But your real goal is to know these by ear: tap the 🔊 and say each word back out loud before you flip.

Tip: adjectives ending in -o/-a agree with the person — alto for a boy, alta for a girl. Words ending in -e or a consonant (fuerte, valiente, joven) don't change for gender. And trabajador adds -a for the feminine. Describe with ser.

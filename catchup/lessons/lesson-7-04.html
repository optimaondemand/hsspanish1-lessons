★ Aplicación — Putting the Adjectives to Work
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the whole system. Today you use it — in writing and by ear.
This is your second encounter with the full adjective system, landing a day after the big lesson on purpose — spacing is how agreement becomes automatic. There's no new grammar today. You'll make each adjective agree yourself in a short guided writing, then train your ear with a five-item dictation drawn from yesterday's clips. It's all ungraded practice: mistakes here are exactly how the habit gets built.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to complete a sentence with the correctly agreeing form of an adjective and explain why.
2
I will be able to listen to a spoken describing phrase and write down what I hear, accents included.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 5 min

The system, in one breath

Before you write, say the shortcut out loud: an adjective agrees with the person — -o/-a words change for gender and number (four forms); -e/consonant words change only for number (two forms); -or words add -a for the feminine. And the adjective goes after the noun. Hold that in your head — you're about to make the calls yourself.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
After yesterday's big lesson, which piece feels least automatic right now?
✍️ PART 2 · ESCRITURA GUIADA 15 min

You make it agree

On paper or in a document, copy each sentence and write the correct form of the adjective in parentheses. Say why to yourself as you write — which family, which gender, singular or plural. Commit to all six before you reveal the key. No peeking early; the thinking is the practice.

Three families

-o/-a: four forms (alto/a/os/as)
-e / consonant: two forms (fuerte/fuertes)
-or: adds -a (trabajador → trabajadora)

Two reminders

Use ser to describe (es, son…).
Put the adjective after the noun.
joven → jóvenes (accent!)
  1. Mi hermana es muy ______ (generoso).
  2. Mis primos son ______ (alto).
  3. Ella es una chica ______ (valiente).
  4. Mi abuelo es ______ (trabajador).
  5. Los estudiantes son ______ (joven).
  6. Es una casa ______ (bonito).
1. generosa — -o/-a word, one female → -a. (Mi hermana es muy generosa.)
2. altos — -o/-a word, male/mixed group → -os. (Mis primos son altos.)
3. valiente — ends in -e: no separate feminine, no change. (una chica valiente.)
4. trabajador — one male → the base -or form, no -a. (Mi abuelo es trabajador.)
5. jóvenes — plural of joven: add -es and the written accent. (Los estudiantes son jóvenes.)
6. bonita — -o/-a word agreeing with casa (feminine), after the noun. (Es una casa bonita.)
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +6 min
Write two sentences of your own about real people — one describing a single person and one describing a group — using at least one adjective from each family. Read them aloud and check every ending.
🎧 PART 3 · DICTADO 12 min

Listen and write — five clips

These are the model clips from yesterday's grammar lesson — now the training wheels are off. For each one: click 🔊 (play it as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear, mind the accents, then reveal to check. Don't reveal until you've written your best guess.

1
Write the four forms you hear…
alto · alta · altos · altas. The full four-form set of an -o/-a adjective — gender, then number.
2
Write what you hear…
Él es fuerte. Ella es fuerte. fuerte ends in -e — the same word for a boy and a girl.
3
Write what you hear…
trabajador · trabajadora. An -or adjective: the feminine adds -a.
4
Write the plurals you hear…
altos · fuertes · trabajadores · jóvenes. Note the accent that appears on jóvenes.
5
Write what you hear…
una casa bonita. Noun first, adjective after — and bonita agrees with casa.

Notice the pattern your ear is learning: the ending of the adjective always answers to the noun — and it comes after it.

🧠 PART 4 · ¿CONCUERDA? 8 min

Two to finish on

Same question every time: which family is this, and does the ending match the person?

✅ TRY IT · make it agree
“Mis tías son ______.” (My aunts — all women — are funny.)
✅ TRY IT · make it agree
“Mi madre es ______.” (My mother is patient.)

One to build yourself: how would you say “a strong, honest man”? Say it out loud first, then reveal.

un hombre fuerte y honesto. Noun first; fuerte (Family 2, no change) and honesto (Family 1, -o for one male) both after the noun.

Catch-up Lesson A
★ Un, una, unos, unas — The Words for “A” and “Some”
Lesson · about 30 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You already know four words for “the”. Today you learn the four words for “a” and “some” — and they follow the same two questions.
In Lesson 0.05 you learned el, la, los, las. When you mean a book instead of the book, Spanish switches to un, una, unos, unas. You choose them exactly the way you choose el or la: is the noun masculine or feminine, and is it one or more than one? Because you learned every noun glued to its article, you already know which one to pick.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose un, una, unos, or unas for a noun and explain why.
2
I will be able to leave out un / una when I say who someone is with ser (Soy estudiante).
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 4 min

“The” book or “a” book?

In English you switch between the and a all the time without thinking: “I need a backpack” (any backpack) versus “I need the backpack” (one particular backpack). Spanish makes the same switch.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
How do el and la feel to you now?
📚 PART 2 · UN / UNA 8 min

“A” or “an” — for one thing

If the noun takes el, “a” is un. If it takes la, “a” is una. Listen, then say each one out loud.

  • un libroa book  (el libro)
  • un examena test  (el examen)
  • una mochilaa backpack  (la mochila)
  • una clasea class  (la clase)

Now in a sentence with ser: es means “it is.”

  • Es un libro.It's a book.
  • Es una mochila.It's a backpack.
📚 PART 3 · UNOS / UNAS 8 min

“Some” — for more than one

For more than one, add -s: unos goes with los nouns, unas with las nouns. In English it usually means “some.” Here is the whole picture, “the” and “a / some” side by side:

MasculineFeminine
One · “the”el librola mochila
One · “a”un librouna mochila
More · “the”los libroslas mochilas
More · “some”unos librosunas mochilas
  • unos librossome books
  • unas mochilassome backpacks
  • unos amigossome friends (boys, or a mixed group)
  • unas amigassome friends (all girls)
  • Son unos libros.They're some books.
  • Son unas amigas.They're some friends.

Notice unos amigos: a group of boys, or a mixed group of boys and girls, takes the masculine form. Only a group of all girls takes unas amigas.

💡 PART 4 · CUANDO NO SE USA 5 min

When Spanish leaves “a” out

English says “I am a student.” Spanish does not. When you use ser to say who someone is — a role like student or teacher, or a nationality — there is no un or una. You have already seen this in every lesson since Módulo 1:

  • Soy estudiante.I am a student. (not soy un estudiante)
  • Ella es maestra.She is a teacher. (not es una maestra)

For things, keep it: Es un libro. For who a person is, drop it: Soy estudiante.

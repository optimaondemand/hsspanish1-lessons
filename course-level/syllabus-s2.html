About Semester 2

Acciones y experiencias — Actions & Experiences

Semester 2 puts Spanish into motion. You already know how to describe people, places, and feelings in the present. Now you'll say what you're going to do, talk about actions with a wider set of verbs, describe your daily routine, and — the big new step at the end — talk about the past: what you did.

You'll also travel further across the Spanish-speaking world — Argentina, Chile, deeper into Mexico, Spain, and the Andes — through music, food, cities, and festivals.

Building on Semester 1: Semester 2 assumes you own the S1 grammar — ser, estar, tener, gustar, regular present-tense verbs, and adjective agreement. Module 1 spirals it back in before adding anything new.
Communicative Goals

What You Will Learn in Semester 2

  • Say what you're going to do (ir a + infinitive)
  • Use stem-changing verbs (e→ie, o→ue, e→i)
  • Use common irregular "yo" verbs (hago, pongo, salgo…)
  • Describe your daily routine with reflexive verbs
  • Talk about the city, giving and following directions
  • Use saber and conocer, and object pronouns
  • Talk about the past: the preterite of regular verbs
  • Use the preterite of high-frequency irregular verbs
  • Compare things and describe what's happening right now
  • Narrate a real experience combining present and past

Grammar You Will Study

More present tense: ir a + infinitive (near future) · stem-changing verbs · irregular yo forms · reflexive verbs · saber vs. conocer · direct-object pronouns · present progressive (estar + -ando/-iendo) · comparatives

The past (a gentle on-ramp): the preterite of regular -ar/-er/-ir verbs, plus the highest-frequency irregular preterites (ser/ir, hacer, tener, estar) — enough to tell simple stories about what you did.
Note on the past tense: Spanish 1 introduces the preterite gently — regular verbs first, then a few key irregulars — so you can narrate real experiences. The full past-tense system (all irregulars, the imperfect, and the preterite/imperfect contrast) is the focus of Spanish 2.
